Protecting the Big Blue
  • Over 50 prominent international and domestic marine and environmental non-governmental organisations, eminent scientists and world-leading universities have united to #BacktheBlueBelt and advocate for marine protected areas (MPAs).
  • The Government of South Georgia and the South Sandwich Islands has announced amendments to their MPA. This has not resulted in full protection of the waters around these uninhabited South Atlantic islands, despite support from the UK public, 285 MPs, scientists, businesses and conservationists.
  • The first Marine Science Symposium focusing on the 644,000-km2 British Indian Ocean Territory was recently held at the Royal Geographical Society. (Check out the summary and videos)

  • The International Union for Conservation of Nature (IUCN)’s MPA standards are being reissued and will be released in 2019, emphasising that MPAs should be “conservation focused with nature as the priority”.
