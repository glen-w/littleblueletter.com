Put some wind in their sails!
Two inspiring projects by young ocean leaders need your help to meet their crowdfunding goals:
  • Sea the Future is a one-year interdisciplinary sailing expedition around Northwestern Europe that will explore the human-ocean relationship.
  • Bigger than Us is a short animated movie inspired by Inuit legends that will take you on a journey to understand the problem of marine pollution. The film explores the impacts of our way of life and shows what we can do in a friendly and understandable way.
Meanwhile, this fantastic animation about the high seas has been nominated for an award (so please go vote!).
