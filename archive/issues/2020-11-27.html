Bigfin squid sightings in Australia
  • Bigfin squid (Magnapinna sp.) are one of the most cryptic animals in the deep ocean.
  • They occupy the bathypelagic zone (1,000 to 4,000 m depth) and are characterised by their unusually long arms and tentacles, and reach a total length ranging from 2 to 7 m.
  • Only a dozen sightings have been recorded worldwide, often kilometres underwater, with no adult specimens ever obtained.
  • The recent observations recorded in the Great Australian Bight are even more exciting, with multiple sightings being recorded within the same area in a short time frame.
  • This was also the first time these animals have been measured using paired lasers, giving a more accurate picture of their size.

#OceanOptimism
  • More than 100 short finned pilot whales were saved from stranding by volunteers in Sri Lanka. Although this species is known to strand in large numbers frequently, the community effort to refloat the animals took over 16 hours and was successful.
  • Tristan da Cunha has announced that 700, 000 sq. km of its waters, would be designated a Marine Protected Area (MPA), the fourth largest of its kind. The tiny island in the South Atlantic boasts with rockhopper colonies, sevengill sharks, yellow-nosed albatross and many more species that the new MPA aims to protect.

Underwater wine
  • Did you know there is such a thing as sea-water infused wine? Dating back to the ancient Greece, grapes were sunk in the sea prior to fermentation to make a sweet wine on the island of Chios.
  • Attilio Scienza and Antonio Arrighi are trying to replicate the method, producing the first 40 bottles in 2019. The result was a wine “with a scent of Mediterranean herbs and an aroma of honey and almond”.
