Ocean update
  • The EU made 22 new commitments for ocean action at the recent Our Ocean conference in Oslo, worth almost €540 million. The commitments target science, pollution, "blue economy" and international cooperation.​ 
  • More info on all the commitments made at Our Ocean is available here, including an interactive map.
  • Humpback whale population on the rise after near miss with extinction.
  • Eight Urgent Steps for Our Ocean Planet from World Ocean Radio (listen or read).
  • New evidence that the first cells might have formed at the bottom of the ocean.

Put wind in their sails!
Two inspiring projects by young ocean leaders need your help to meet their crowdfunding goals:
  • Sea the Future is a one-year interdisciplinary sailing expedition around Northwestern Europe that will explore the human-ocean relationship.
  • ​Bigger than Us is a short animated movie inspired by Inuit legends that will take you on a journey to understand the problem of marine pollution. The film explores the impacts of our way of life and shows what we can do in a friendly and understandable way.
