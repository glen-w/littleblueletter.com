<div style="font-style: normal; font-size: 16px; color: rgb(85, 85, 85);"><span style="font-weight: bolder; font-style: normal; font-size: 16px;">High time for the high seas</span><br/>
<span style="font-weight: 400; font-style: normal; font-size: 16px;">As summer draws to a close, the international community gathered in the windowless halls of UN headquarters in New York to take an historic step on </span><a href="http://bit.ly/2LQR2B6" style="font-size: 16px; font-style: normal; font-weight: 400;">Long and Winding Road</a><span style="font-size: 16px; font-style: normal; font-weight: 400;"> towards a new agreement that could protect the <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5qw8bAdIS_E" style="font-size: 16px;">dumbo octopus</a>, the <a href="https://www.popularmechanics.com/science/animals/a23068148/scientists-discover-trippy-new-translucent-fish-at-the-bottom-of-the-ocean/" style="font-size: 16px;">recently discovered pink, blue and purple translucent snail fish</a>, and all the other wonders waiting beyond national waters. </span><span style="font-size: 16px; font-style: normal; font-weight: 400;">You can catch up on the action with the </span><a href="http://enb.iisd.org/oceans/bbnj/igc1/about.html" style="font-size: 16px; font-style: normal; font-weight: 400;">Earth Negotiations Bulletin</a><span style="font-size: 16px; font-style: normal; font-weight: 400;"> and dive into the details with the </span><a href="http://highseasalliance.org/treatytracker/" style="font-size: 16px; font-style: normal; font-weight: 400;">High Seas Alliance Treaty Tracker</a><span style="font-size: 16px; font-style: normal; font-weight: 400;">. </span></div>

<hr/><span style="font-weight: bolder; font-style: normal; font-size: 16px;">A 1977 perspective on sea-bed mining</span><br/>
<span style="font-size: 16px; font-style: normal; caret-color: rgb(85, 85, 85);">Forty years ago, a writer for the </span><a href="https://www.nytimes.com/1977/07/17/archives/mining-the-wealth-of-the-ocean-deep-multinational-companies-are.html?url=http%3A%2F%2Ftimesmachine.nytimes.com%2Ftimesmachine%2F1977%2F07%2F17%2F356544562.html%3Faction%3Dclick®ion=ArchiveBody&amp;module=LedeAsset&amp;pgtype=article&amp;contentCollection=Archives" style="font-size: 16px; font-style: normal; caret-color: rgb(85, 85, 85);">New York Times</a><span style="font-size: 16px; font-style: normal; caret-color: rgb(85, 85, 85);"> said that seabed mining was, "one of the most bitterest - and, some observers say, most hopeless disputes in intern</span><span style="font-size: 16px; font-style: normal; caret-color: rgb(85, 85, 85);">ational relations." The Law of the Sea Convention (1982) was still half a decade away, and mining companies, eager to get to work, complained that "progress at the U</span><span style="font-size: 16px; font-style: normal; caret-color: rgb(85, 85, 85);">N is</span><span style="font-size: 16px; font-style: normal; caret-color: rgb(85, 85, 85);"> slower than the growth of </span><a href="https://www.google.com/search?q=manganese+nodules&amp;client=safari&amp;rls=en&amp;source=lnms&amp;tbm=isch&amp;sa=X&amp;ved=0ahUKEwjlo6eYl7vdAhXsm-AKHcqJDJMQ_AUICigB&amp;biw=1440&amp;bih=839" style="font-size: 16px; font-style: normal; caret-color: rgb(85, 85, 85);">manganese nodules</a><span style="font-size: 16px; font-style: normal; caret-color: rgb(85, 85, 85);">.</span><span style="font-size: 16px; font-style: normal; caret-color: rgb(85, 85, 85);"> P</span><span style="font-size: 16px; font-style: normal; caret-color: rgb(85, 85, 85);">olitics aside, </span><span style="font-size: 16px; font-style: normal; caret-color: rgb(85, 85, 85);">extraction itself seemed rather daunting: "One mining expert compares his company's process to picking something off the sidewalk by dangling a long piece of spaghetti from the top of the World Trade Center."</span>
<div style='font-weight: 400; font-style: normal; font-size: 12px; font-family: "Helvetica Neue"; line-height: normal; font-stretch: normal; color: rgb(69, 69, 69);'><span style="font-style: normal; font-size: 16px; caret-color: rgb(85, 85, 85); color: rgb(85, 85, 85);"><img src="/images/letters/inferred-2018-09-12-2-ezgif-4-082db9bb52.gif" style="width: 534px; max-width: 534px;display:block;float:none;clear:both;height:auto;max-width:100%;margin:0.75rem auto;" width="534"/></span></div>
<div style='font-weight: 400; font-style: normal; font-size: 12px; font-family: "Helvetica Neue"; line-height: normal; font-stretch: normal; color: rgb(69, 69, 69); text-align: center;'><span style="font-style: normal; font-size: 16px; caret-color: rgb(85, 85, 85); color: rgb(85, 85, 85);"><em>The modern equivalent of the dangling spaghetti. The proposed use of these monstrosities to mine the seafloor understandably has many worried that there will be irrevocable damage to delicate benthic ecosystems.</em></span></div>
<hr style="caret-color: rgb(85, 85, 85);"/><span style="font-weight: bolder; font-size: 16px; font-style: normal;">Adventurers</span><br/>
<span style="font-weight: 400; font-style: normal; font-size: 16px; caret-color: rgb(85, 85, 85);">In 1947, Thor Heyerdahl floated for 101 days in the <em style="font-size: 16px;">Kon-Tiki </em>raft, drifting from Peru and across the Pacific towards Polynesia in a quest to show that South Americans could have settled the islands. The <a href="https://vimeo.com/49401742" style="font-size: 16px;">documentary <em style="font-size: 16px;">Kon-Tiki</em></a>, which won an Oscar in 1951, shows the crew of six catching flying fish and diving in a bamboo cage with the sharks (and not a single piece of plastic in sight). <br/>

<hr/><span style="font-weight: bolder; font-style: normal; font-size: 16px;">From the editor</span><br/>
<span style="font-weight: 400; font-style: normal; font-size: 16px;">The International Whaling Commission (IWC) is </span><a href="http://enb.iisd.org/iwc/67/" style="font-weight: 400; font-style: normal; font-size: 16px;">meeting</a><span style="font-weight: 400; font-style: normal; font-size: 16px;"> in Brazil, as scientists in the Pacific Northwest of the United States ponder </span><a href="https://www.theatlantic.com/science/archive/2018/09/dolphins-tail-walking-trend/569314/" style="font-weight: 400; font-style: normal; font-size: 16px;">whether whales are grieving</a><span style="font-weight: 400; font-style: normal; font-size: 16px;"> their dead. A once-captive dolphin </span><a href="https://www.theatlantic.com/science/archive/2018/09/dolphins-tail-walking-trend/569314/" style="font-weight: 400; font-style: normal; font-size: 16px;">taught its friends</a><span style="font-weight: 400; font-style: normal; font-size: 16px;"> to tail walk. Despite the dramatic decline in fisheries worldwide, one in every three fish caught </span><a href="https://www.theguardian.com/environment/2018/jul/09/one-in-three-fish-caught-never-makes-it-to-the-plate-un-report" style="font-weight: 400; font-style: normal; font-size: 16px;">never makes it on to a plate</a><span style="font-weight: 400; font-style: normal; font-size: 16px;">. The International Oceanographic Commission is holding a meeting this week to plan the UN </span><a href="https://en.unesco.org/ocean-decade" style="font-weight: 400; font-style: normal; font-size: 16px;"><em style="font-size: 16px;">Decade of Ocean Science for Sustainable Development</em></a><span style="font-weight: 400; font-style: normal; font-size: 16px;"> (2021-2030). Thanks so much to all the dedicated folks that are pushing for a strong agreement for the conservation and sustainable use of the high seas - you are my heroes! And special thanks to Isabel, our phenomenal intern who sadly leaves us next week - the IDDRI ocean team will miss you!</span>
