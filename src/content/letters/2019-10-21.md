<hr/><strong>Ocean update</strong>
<ul>
<li><a href="https://www.theguardian.com/environment/2019/oct/24/offshore-windfarms-can-provide-more-electricity-than-the-world-needs">Offshore windfarms</a> 'can provide more electricity than the world needs'.</li>
<li>Only four of the UK's 355 marine protected areas are fully protected from fishing and other activities, but a <a href="https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/highly-protected-marine-areas-hpmas-review-2019/highly-protected-marine-areas-review-terms-of-reference">review is underway</a>. British readers can <a href="https://www.mcsuk.org/campaigns/hpma-home?pk_campaign=20191015_HPMA">sign the petition</a> to strengthen protection.</li>
<li>It's International River Dolphin Day! Find out how <a href="http://wwf.panda.org/wwf_news/?354832/Conserving-the-worlds-endangered-river-dolphins-takes-cutting-edge-science-and-community-rescues">cutting-edge tech and community action</a> is helping conserve these charismatic marine mammals. Loads more info in <a href="https://whales.org/wp-content/uploads/2018/08/small-cetaceans-big-problems.pdf">this report</a>.</li>
</ul>

<hr/><strong>Put some wind in their sails!</strong><br/>
Two inspiring projects by young ocean leaders need your help to meet their crowdfunding goals:
<ul>
<li><em><strong><a href="https://ekosea.com/en/crowdfunding/375-expedition-sea-the-future">Sea the Future</a></strong></em> is a one-year interdisciplinary sailing expedition around Northwestern Europe that will explore the human-ocean relationship.</li>
</ul>
<img src="/images/letters/inferred-2019-11-04-3-grouppic.jpg" style="font-size: 16px; font-style: normal; width: 508px; max-width: 508px;display:block;float:none;clear:both;height:auto;max-width:100%;margin:0.75rem auto;" width="508"/>
<ul>
<li><em><a href="https://www.indiegogo.com/projects/bigger-than-us-the-short-animated-film">Bigger than Us</a></em> is a short animated movie inspired by Inuit legends that will take you on a journey to understand the problem of marine pollution. The film explores the impacts of our way of life and shows what we can do in a friendly and understandable way.</li>
</ul>
