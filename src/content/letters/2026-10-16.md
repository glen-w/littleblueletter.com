<p><strong>🌊 Geoengineering finally gets a rulebook</strong><br/>You can't just dump iron in the ocean and call it a climate solution! That's what countries at the International Maritime Organization (IMO) <a href="https://www.imo.org/en/mediacentre/pressbriefings/pages/imo-adopts-marine-geoengineering-frameworks-for-research-activities.aspx">agreed this week</a> (sort of). They adopted new frameworks for assessing marine geoengineering research proposals, grounded in a precautionary approach. Proposals must demonstrate a legitimate scientific purpose, with thorough risk assessment, stakeholder consultation, peer review, monitoring and reporting.</p>

<p><strong>🌀 Momentum building for protection of the Sargasso Sea</strong><br/>A new <a href="https://www.gov.bm/news/uk-minister-for-the-overseas-territories-to-visit-bermuda">Hamilton Declaration</a> will be signed next week in Bermuda. It's a step towards international cooperation on high-seas biodiversity, and bolsters a future collective proposal for protections under the BBNJ Agreement.</p>

<p><strong>💰 Securing long-term financing for the Western Indian Ocean</strong><br/>At a meeting of the Nairobi Convention (which is celebrating its <a href="https://www.nairobiconvention.org/news/cop12-40th-anniversary-western-indian-ocean">40th birthday</a>!), countries <a href="https://www.project-syndicate.org/commentary/ocean-conservation-trust-fund-to-facilitate-cross-border-cooperation-by-marie-may-jeremie-2026-10">decided to develop</a> a regional conservation trust fund to build sustainable funding beyond the usual piecemeal efforts. Because, as Seychelles Environment Minister Marie-May Jeremie put it: <em>"Reefs do not recover according to project deadlines."</em></p>
<img src="/images/letters/261016_turtle.jpg" alt="Sea turtle" width="600" style="width:600px;max-width:100%;height:auto;"/>

<hr/>
<p><strong>🐋 China protects habitat for Bryde's whales</strong><br/>The waters off Weizhou Island now have a dedicated 335 km² Bryde's whale habitat, with fishing banned for most of the year. It's a first for large-whale habitat protection on China's coast. <a href="https://www.ecns.cn/m/cns-wire/2026-10-13/detail-ihfmcrte5284873.shtml">ECNS</a></p>

<p>🇨🇳 China is in ocean news a lot lately, <a href="https://www.msn.com/en-za/news/other/exclusive-china-proposes-its-own-antarctic-marine-protected-area-in-a-soviet-named-sea/ar-AA2dHGeU">proposing a new Antarctic protected area</a> (which has raised a few eyebrows, given their longstanding reluctance to agree to other MPAs in the region), planning a <a href="https://www.msn.com/en-au/news/australia/chinas-plan-to-build-sixth-antarctic-base-raises-concerns/ar-AA2d378s">new Antarctic base</a> and vying to host the BBNJ secretariat.</p>
