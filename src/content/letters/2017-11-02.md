<hr style="color: rgb(85, 85, 85); text-align: center;"/>
<div style="font-weight: normal; font-style: normal; font-size: 16px; color: rgb(85, 85, 85);">Blue Planet 2 is so incredible that I'm compelled to once again sing its praises. Attenborough's team continued to amaze on Sunday, plunging to the darkest depths of our planet to show us <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Yn574acq9Fk">sharks feeding on a whale carcass</a> and an <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZwuVpNYrKPY">eel writhing in toxic shock</a> after venturing into an underwater saltwater lake (the deep sea is a weird place indeed). Deep sea scientists have also discovered a new type of hunting - '<a href="https://www.nytimes.com/2017/11/01/science/nudibranch-sea-slug-kleptopredation.html">kleptopredation</a>' (i.e. eating something that has just eaten something).<br/>
<br/>
<img src="/images/letters/inferred-2017-11-02-1-dnkusqkuiaargx.jpg" style="width: 195px; max-width: 195px;display:block;float:none;clear:both;height:auto;max-width:100%;margin:0.75rem auto;" width="195"/>The <a href="http://www.france24.com/en/20171103-fiji-bonn-germany-cop23-united-nations-climate-change-conference">South Pacific comes to Germany</a> this week, with Fiji hosting <a href="http://newsroom.unfccc.int/cop-23-bonn/">COP23 in Bonn</a> (IISD coverage <a href="http://sdg.iisd.org/events/unfccc-cop-23/">here</a>). The meeting takes place during one of the <a href="http://www.newsweek.com/year-warm-2017-track-be-least-third-warmest-year-record-703349">hottest years on record</a> and just after the US pulled out of the Paris Accord (Nicaragua and Syria just joined, leaving the US completely isolated). <a href="https://www.independent.co.uk/news/world/politics/cop23-un-latest-climate-change-trump-us-issues-what-will-happen-talks-a8041146.html">Here's</a> what to expect.<br/>
<br/>
Fiji is a dedicated ocean advocate (see <a href="https://cop23.com.fj/fighting-ocean-pollution-and-climate-change-is-a-two-front-war/">here</a> &amp; <a href="http://fijitimes.com/story.aspx?id=417307">here</a>), so our seas are getting <a href="https://www.newsdeeply.com/oceans/articles/2017/11/06/a-new-push-to-make-ocean-issues-a-priority-as-u-n-climate-talks-begin">more airtime than ever</a> at these climate talks: many stakeholders have united behind the <a href="https://ocean-climate.org/?lang=en">Ocean &amp; Climate Platform</a>, a nine-point policy framework for integrating ocean and climate action; <a href="https://rocainitiative.files.wordpress.com/2017/11/final-program-oceans-action-day-at-cop-23-email-november-41.pdf">Oceans Action Day</a> (tomorrow) will bring together high level policymakers and scientists to advance ocean issues; and a signing ceremony will be held for the <em><a href="http://www.iddri.org/Themes/Because-the-Ocean-ENG.pdf">Because the Ocean</a></em> declaration, which calls on countries to include the ocean in their national climate plans.<br/>
<br/>
November is Manatee Awareness Month! <a href="http://oceana.org/blog/what-you-didn%E2%80%99t-know-about-manatees-honor-manatee-awareness-month">Here</a> is a bunch of interesting stuff that you might not know about these gentle giants. <span style="font-size: 16px; font-style: normal; font-weight: normal;">Click </span><a href="https://gift.oceana.org/content/manatee-plush-adoption" style="font-size: 16px; font-style: normal; font-weight: normal;">here</a><span style="font-size: 16px; font-style: normal; font-weight: normal;"> to symbolically adopt one (comes with a cute plush toy!), </span><span style="font-size: 16px; font-style: normal; font-weight: normal;">and <a href="http://www.wired.com/2014/01/absurd-creature-of-the-week-stellers-sea-cow/">here</a> to read the fascinating story</span><span style="font-size: 16px; font-style: normal; font-weight: normal;"> of the (long extinct) 12-ton Steller's Sea Cow.</span></div>
