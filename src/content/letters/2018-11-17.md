<div style="font-weight: 400; font-style: normal; font-size: 16px; color: rgb(85, 85, 85);"><strong>Wetlands in the world spotlight</strong><br/>
The <a href="https://www.ramsar.org/">Ramsar Convention</a> on Wetlands - an intergovernmental treaty that provides a framework for national action and international cooperation for the conservation and wise use of wetlands and their resources - <a href="http://sdg.iisd.org/events/13th-meeting-of-the-conference-of-the-contracting-parties-to-the-ramsar-convention-on-wetlands-cop13/">recently met</a> in Dubai. The Convention was established over 40 years ago to protect wetlands around the world and there are now more than 2,000 Ramsar-designated wetlands that represent rare or unique habitats important for conserving biological diversity.<br/>
<img src="/images/letters/inferred-2018-11-17-1-docx-littleblueletter16112018copie-61e64.png" style="width: 417px; max-width: 417px;display:block;float:none;clear:both;height:auto;max-width:100%;margin:0.75rem auto;" width="417"/>The Convention just issued the first-ever <a href="https://www.ramsar.org/news/wetlands-worlds-most-valuable-ecosystem-disappearing-three-times-faster-than-forests-warns-new">global report on the state of the world's wetlands</a>, finding that about 35% of the planet’s wetlands were lost between 1970 and 2015. This is concerning as wetlands mitigate floods, protect coastlines and build community resilience to disasters, and play an important role in transport, tourism and culture. They are also a vital source of food (60%-90% of U.S. commercial fisheries <a href="https://www.nps.gov/subjects/wetlands/why.htm">depend on wetlands</a>) and provide the <a href="https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0272771413000632">largest terrestrial carbon store</a>.<br/>
<br/>
Around the world people are helping us understand the value of wetlands in order to develop better ways of protecting and restoring these important habitats. Recently, a team of interdisciplinary researchers (including the insurance industry!) <a href="https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-017-09269-z">calculated the financial benefits</a> that coastal wetlands provide by reducing damages from hurricanes and offers new evidence that protecting natural ecosystems is a cost-effective way of reducing risks from flooding. Another team of researchers suggests the idea of “<a href="https://phys.org/news/2018-09-professional-everglades.html#nRlv">Wetland Restoration Brokers</a>” - independent mediators that could help settle conflicts among stakeholders or between stakeholders and the environment. And the Ramsar Convention itself has launched <a href="https://www.bbc.com/news/science-environment-46020176">accreditation for cities</a> that conserve wetlands, with 18 cities around the world so far recognized.

<hr/><strong>#OceanOptimism</strong>
<ul>
<li>California Governor Jerry Brown <a href="http://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/billNavClient.xhtml?bill_id=201720180SB1017">signed a law</a> to <a href="https://www.pewtrusts.org/en/research-and-analysis/articles/2018/09/27/california-gov-jerry-brown-signs-bill-to-end-use-of-drift-gillnets">phase out the use of destructive swordfish gillnets</a>. The bill had broad bipartisan support, passing the State Assembly unanimously and the State Senate by a vote of 36-1 before being sent to the governor’s desk.</li>
<li>The European Parliament has voted for a <a href="https://www.bbc.com/news/world-europe-45965605#">complete ban</a> on a range of single-use plastics across the union in a bid to stop pollution of the oceans.</li>
<li>Researchers discover the <a href="https://www.nationalgeographic.com/animals/2018/10/deep-sea-octopus-nursery-discovered-animals-news/">world's largest deep-sea octopus nursery</a> with over 1,000 females.</li>
</ul>
<hr/><strong>More on wetlands</strong>
<ul>
<li>Why a wetland <a href="https://phys.org/news/2018-10-wetland.html">might not be wet</a>: Most Australian wetlands have natural wet-dry cycles, with dry spells that can last for decades. So, if wetlands are still wetlands when they're dry, how do you spot one?</li>
<li>Impacts of human‐induced environmental change in wetlands on aquatic animals: a <a href="https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/brv.12358">review of the ecological impacts</a> of human alterations to wetland ecosystems.</li>
<li>NOAA’s National Estuarine Research Reserve System is comprised of 29 sites designated to protect and study estuarine systems, including a Ramsar-designated wetland (<a href="https://www.noaa.gov/media-release/elkhorn-slough-designated-wetland-of-international-importance">California’s Elkhorn Slough</a>), one of 39 Ramsar sites in the United States.</li>
</ul>
