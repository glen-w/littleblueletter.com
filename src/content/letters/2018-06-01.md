<div style="font-weight: 400; font-style: normal; font-size: 16px; color: rgb(85, 85, 85);"><strong>Longing for beauty?</strong><br/>
You’ll find it in diatoms, single-cell algae that create jewel-like glass shells. <em><a href="https://vimeo.com/90160649">The Diatomist</a> </em>is a marvellous short documentary on the Victorian art of diatom arrangement. You’ll also find it in the singing of whales and icebergs or the silence of the still ocean – hear them in the Alfred-Wegener Institut <a href="http://multimedia.awi.de/public/#1527870088849_1">library of polar sounds</a>.

<hr/><strong>Ocean opportunities</strong>
<ul>
<li><a href="https://www.youmares.org/">YOUMARES Conference</a> for Young Marine Researchers will take place in Oldenburg, Germany in September 2018.</li>
<li>An <a href="http://www.oceanmooc.org/en/">Ocean MOOC</a> offered by GEOMAR (Germany) together with SDG Academy, will rune for the third time in Autumn. This year we are building an entire ocean youth community around it, so we are looking for youth initiatives in ocean sustainability and proactive people in different parts of the globe who would want to contribute to the course.</li>
</ul>
<hr/><strong>From the editor</strong>
<ul>
<li>The 118 organisations of the NGO Tuna Forum have <a href="https://www.savingseafood.org/wp-content/uploads/2018/05/Global-Sustainability-Outreach-Appeal-News-Release-5-15-18.pdf">called on Regional Fisheries Management Organisations (RFMOs) to improve tuna management</a>, requesting specific action on catch monitoring and control, bycatch limits, surveillance, and fish-aggregating devices (FADs).</li>
<li>Congratulations to the winners of the <a href="https://www.boatinternational.com/luxury-yacht-events/ocean-awards/the-ocean-awards-2018--37003/frame-8?view_all=true">Boat Magazine Ocean Awards 2018</a>. </li>
<li><em>The High Seas, What's at Stake? </em><a href="https://www.eventbrite.fr/e/billets-la-haute-mer-quels-enjeux-high-seas-whats-at-stake-44907910770">Conference</a> on 21 June in Paris.</li>
<li>New Zealand's Government dropped long-held plans to restrict deep sea trawling following <a href="https://www.stuff.co.nz/national/politics/104082934/Government-drops-plan-to-restrict-deep-sea-trawling-protect-orange-roughy">aggressive industry lobbying</a>.</li>
<li>From 2000 to 2013, <a href="http://aiddata.org/financing-for-sustainable-development">donors spent</a> $340bn on SDG16 (Peace, justice and strong institutions). They spent just $2.7bn on SDG14.</li>
</ul>
