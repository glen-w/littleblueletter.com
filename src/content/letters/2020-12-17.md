<hr style="font-size: 16px;"/><b style="font-size: 16px;"><a href="https://www.pewtrusts.org/en/research-and-analysis/articles/2018/08/30/in-win-for-marine-life-california-to-end-drift-gillnet-fishing" style="font-size: 16px;">Drift gillnets to be phased out in California</a></b><br/>
<span style="font-size: 16px; text-indent: -18pt;">The swordfish and thresher shark fishery in California will be </span><a href="https://www.sustainweb.org/goodcatch/drift_gill_and_set_nets/" style="font-size: 16px; text-indent: -18pt;">phasing out</a><span style="font-size: 16px; text-indent: -18pt;"> the use of drift gillnets over the course of the next four years. </span><span lang="EN-GB" style="font-size: 1em; text-indent: -18pt;"><a href="https://www.sustainweb.org/goodcatch/drift_gill_and_set_nets/">Drift gillnets</a> can be more than a mile long and reach up to 60 m (200 ft) depth from the sea surface. They are often left overnight, resulting in entanglement of various non-target species.</span><br/>
<br/>
<span lang="EN-GB" style="text-indent: -18pt;"><span style="font-size: 1em;">Around </span><a href="https://seaturtles.org/wp-content/uploads/2018/06/Driftnet-Shark-Report-2016-1.pdf" style="font-size: 1em;">60% of the species</a><span style="font-size: 1em;"> caught by the driftnet fishery are listed as threatened or endangered by the IUCN. </span><span style="font-size: 16px; text-indent: -18pt;">The </span><a href="https://www.feinstein.senate.gov/public/index.cfm/press-releases?id=45a53c06-8693-4bd2-9861-9f506d6e566f&amp;utm_campaign=conservation_uso_oceans_______&amp;utm_source=twitter_trusts&amp;utm_medium=social&amp;utm_content=3rdparty_general____photo_&amp;utm_term=___" style="font-size: 16px; text-indent: -18pt;">law</a><span style="font-size: 16px; text-indent: -18pt;"> that was passed this week aims to replace the drift gillnets with deep-set buoy gear, which will be baited for swordfish and signal to fishermen when a bite is detected.</span><span style="text-indent: -18pt;"><font size="1"> </font></span></span><span lang="EN-GB" style="font-size: 1em; text-indent: -18pt;"><a href="https://www.bmis-bycatch.org/system/files/zotero_attachments/library_1/KM8I6J8L%20-%20Sepulveda%20and%20Aalbers%20-%202018%20-%20Exempted%20Testing%20of%20Deep-set%20Buoy%20Gear%20and%20Concurr.pdf">Trials</a> of the deep-set buoy gear have shown catch consisting of over 80% swordfish compared with traditional drift gillnet gear.</span><span lang="EN-GB" style="text-indent: -18pt;"><font size="1"> </font></span><span lang="EN-GB" style="font-size: 16px; text-indent: -18pt;">Under <a href="https://codes.findlaw.com/ca/fish-and-game-code/fgc-sect-8583.html">the new law</a>, fishermen will receive a $100,000 one-off payment to surrender their nets to be destroyed, and if they choose to surrender their permit a further $10,000.<img src="/images/letters/inferred-2020-12-17-1-1driftnets.jpg" style="width: 640px; max-width: 640px;display:block;float:none;clear:both;height:auto;max-width:100%;margin:0.75rem auto;" width="640"/> </span>
