<hr style="color: rgb(85, 85, 85); text-align: center;"/>
<div style="font-weight: 400; font-style: normal; font-size: 16px; color: rgb(85, 85, 85);"><span style="font-weight: bolder; font-style: normal; font-size: 16px;">Protecting the big blue</span>
<ul dir="ltr">
<li>Spain <a href="https://eu.oceana.org/en/press-center/press-releases/spain-create-second-largest-marine-national-park-mediterranean-south">will create</a> the second-largest marine national park in the Mediterranean, making the Cabrera National Park, south of Majorca, a massive nine times bigger. The park will be afforded the highest level of legal protection.</li>
<li>The government of Chile created two new marine protected areas and one national park last year and 42% of its seas now under some degree of protection. Chile’s newest marine protected area is the <a href="https://www.pewtrusts.org/en/research-and-analysis/articles/2019/01/22/chile-announces-new-marine-protected-area">Diego Ramírez-Drake Passage Marine Park</a>. See some breathtaking images from other <a href="https://www.pewtrusts.org/en/research-and-analysis/articles/2019/01/24/protection-of-chilean-patagonia-grew-in-2018-but-work-remains">Chilean reserves</a>.</li>
<li>The winter wetland of <a href="http://www.birdlife.org/worldwide/news/mud-glorious-mud-spoonie%E2%80%99s-winter-wetland-declared-protected-area">Spoonie</a> (aka the critically endangered Spoon-billed Sandpiper), Nanthar Island in Myanmar, has been declared a protected area.</li>
</ul>
<img src="/images/letters/inferred-2019-02-06-1-2019-02-05012956-christianmilleroninstag.png" style="width: 240px; max-width: 240px;display:block;float:none;clear:both;height:auto;max-width:100%;margin:0.75rem auto;" width="240"/>
<div style="text-align: center;"><em>Young hand-reared spoon-billed sandpiper (© Nicky Hiscock)</em></div>
<ul dir="ltr">
<li>In the UK, <a href="https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-018-38270-3">DNA barcoding</a> is being used to investigate sales of protected shark products in fish and chip shops!</li>
<li>The <a href="https://globalinitiative.net/launch-of-the-iuu-fishing-index/">Global IUU Fishing Index</a> has just been launched, shining a spotlight on fishy fishing practices.​</li>
<li><a href="https://www.cites.org/eng/news/focusing-on-marine-species-for-the-first-time-the-next-world-wildlife-day-is-bound-to-make-a-splash_16112018">World Wildlife Day</a> (3 March) <span style="color: rgb(85, 85, 85); font-size: 16px; font-style: normal; font-weight: 400;">will focus on marine species for the first time.</span></li>
</ul>

<hr/><span style="font-weight: bolder; font-style: normal; font-size: 16px;">#OceanOptimism</span>
<ul dir="ltr">
<li>A sixth grade class in land-locked Arkansas heard about a <a href="https://www.theguardian.com/environment/2015/may/03/starfish-sea-star-deaths-west-coast">mass die-off of starfish on the west coast</a>, so they raised money for <a href="http://www.theguardian.com/environment/2019/jan/30/starfish-sea-stars-west-coast-dying-off-ecosystem-study">research</a> at Cornell University. The findings have just been published in <a href="http://advances.sciencemag.org/content/5/1/eaau7042">Science Advances</a>.</li>
<li>The Ocean was high on the agenda at the <a href="https://www.weforum.org/events/world-economic-forum-annual-meeting/sessions/taking-action-for-the-ocean">World Economic Forum</a> in January. The Friends of Ocean Action group was behind the Ocean Day at Davos with <a href="https://www.weforum.org/friends-of-ocean-action/learn-more">many ocean related panel discussions</a>, including the one titled “<a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=z_hXX7ffQEU&amp;feature=player_embedded">The wild wet west: The high seas: an emerging opportunity or a failed state?</a>” </li>
<li>A successful <a href="https://www.scmp.com/magazines/post-magazine/long-reads/article/2183633/could-hong-kongs-hardy-corals-be-key-saving">coral conservation project</a> in Hong Kong could potentially transform the seas of southern China and beyond.</li>
<li><a href="https://news.sky.com/story/sonar-makes-beaked-whales-suicidal-and-now-we-know-why-11622201">Scientists have figured out</a> why exposure to naval sonar drives beaked whales to beach themselves in bizarre bouts of apparently suicidal behaviour.</li>
</ul>
