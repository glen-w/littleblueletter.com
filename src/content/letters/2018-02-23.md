<div style="color: rgb(85, 85, 85); font-size: 16px; font-style: normal; text-align: left;"><b>Turtle power</b></div>
<div style="color: rgb(85, 85, 85); font-size: 16px; font-style: normal; font-weight: normal; text-align: left;">The 38th <a href="https://internationalseaturtlesociety.org/">International Sea Turtle Symposium</a> kicked off this week in Kobe, Japan. The symposium began with a <a href="https://twitter.com/GoWithTheTrack/status/965747446717456384">traditional prayer</a>, led by a Shinto priest. Participants have already heard about how relocating turtle eggs to hatcheries in Malaysia has slowed population decline, and how employing poachers to protect nesting sites can be a win-win: providing gainful employment, while reducing the risk of turtle nests being plundered. <img src="/images/letters/inferred-2018-02-23-1-tumblrmqxrjco8iz1qinbs8o1400.gif" style="font-weight: normal; font-style: normal; font-size: 16px; outline-color: rgb(236, 33, 38); text-align: center; width: 444px; max-width: 444px;display:block;float:none;clear:both;height:auto;max-width:100%;margin:0.75rem auto;" width="444"/></div>
<p style="color: rgb(85, 85, 85); font-size: 16px; font-style: normal; font-weight: normal; text-align: left;">This is just a taste of possible solutions to conservation problems being offered up for discussion. You can follow along at <a href="http://twitter.com/search?vertical=default&amp;q=%23ISTS38_Kobe&amp;src=typd">#ISTS38_Kobe</a>, where turtle enthusiasts around the globe will be live tweeting various sessions (including a <a href="http://twitter.com/mgodfreyST/status/966575795169763329">scientist wearing flippers</a> while giving a presentation).</p>

<div style="color: rgb(85, 85, 85); font-size: 16px; font-style: normal; font-weight: normal;"><span style="font-weight: bolder; font-size: 16px;">Little blue action</span>
<ul>
<li>Got a dog? Upgrade your nylon leash for an eco friendly <a href="http://amzn.to/2EKZymt">hemp leash</a>. (Ed: Also, be aware of the <a href="https://theconversation.com/a-big-pawprint-the-environmental-impact-of-pet-food-74004">environmental "pawprint"</a> of keeping a furry friend)</li>
<li><a href="https://twitter.com/search?q=%232minutebeachclean&amp;src=tyah">#2MinuteBeachClean</a>. One woman in Cornwall spent 2 minutes picking up litter from her local beach, <a href="http://www.bbc.co.uk/blogs/natureuk/entries/64ed3eea-ae99-41ae-b119-9aea4ebfcaf4">every day for a whole year</a>. Imagine if everyone did that!</li>
<li>Bring a bit of nature into your home by <a href="https://bonnieplants.com/library/how-to-grow-herbs-indoors/">planting a herb garden</a>. The visual reminder of where our food originates is a nice way to stay connected to nature.</li>
</ul>

<div style="color: rgb(85, 85, 85); font-size: 16px; font-style: normal; font-weight: normal; text-align: left;"><span style="font-weight: bolder; font-size: 1em;">From the editor</span><br/>
<span style="font-size: 1em;">I just read <a href="https://www.popularmechanics.com/science/health/a26038/the-blood-of-the-crab/" style="font-size: 16px;">this article</a> about horseshoe crabs, and the unregulated harvesting of their unique blue blood for biomedical applications (fascinating, but not for the faint-hearted). Container deposit schemes <a href="https://theconversation.com/amp/deposit-schemes-reduce-drink-containers-in-the-ocean-by-40-91897">reduce marine litter</a> (obviously). <a href="https://www.theguardian.com/environment/2018/feb/22/debt-for-dolphins-seychelles-create-huge-new-marine-parks-in-world-first-finance-scheme">Debt for dolphins</a>: The Seychelles' ambitious plan to designate huge MPAs in exchange for debt relief is coming along nicely. The </span>winners of <a href="http://www.bbc.com/news/in-pictures-43044592">Underwater Photographer of the Year</a> - truly incredible. The overall winner is a panoramic photo of a British World War Two shipwreck, <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SS_Thistlegorm">SS Thistlegorm</a>, loaded with Norton 16H motorbikes.
