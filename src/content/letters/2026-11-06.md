<p><strong>🌏 Asia Pacific Ocean Week</strong><br/>Last week, 26 to 30 October, Bangkok 🇹🇭 dressed itself up to welcome the first <a href="https://www.unescap.org/events/2026/asia-pacific-ocean-week">Asia-Pacific Ocean Week</a>.</p>
<p>Organised by the UN Economic and Social Commission for Asia and the Pacific (ESCAP), in collaboration with 27 partners, the Ocean Week brought together 450 participants from over 30 countries, representing governments, UN entities, academia, think tanks, NGOs, youth and the private sector.</p>
<img src="/images/letters/261106_apow_banner.png" alt="Asia-Pacific Ocean Week banner" width="400" style="display:block;width:400px;max-width:100%;height:auto;margin:0 auto;"/>
<p>With two plenary sessions, 23 thematic sessions and 11 exhibition booths, the event provided opportunities for the participants to share knowledge and explore ways to strengthen ocean governance and the sustainable use of marine resources across the region.</p>

<hr/>
<p><strong>🌊 Ocean-based climate action in Asia and the Pacific</strong><br/>With COP31 taking place soon in Antalya, Türkiye, a deep dive into the Nationally Determined Contributions (NDCs) of States in Asia and the Pacific revealed just how blue the region's climate commitments are.</p>
<p>Developed by ESCAP, the World Resources Institute and the Ocean and Climate Platform, the report <a href="https://repository.unescap.org/items/59f6fa7c-425a-40a4-ac82-611c9bb3b448"><em>Ocean-based Climate Action in Asia and the Pacific: Status and Ways Forward</em></a> shows that:</p>
<ul>
<li>Among the 38 regional member States, <strong>28 countries, or 74%, include specific commitments related to OBCA.</strong></li>
<li>For <strong>coastal and island nations,</strong> this rises to <strong>90%</strong>.</li>
<li><strong>The implementation of 64% of ocean-based climate NDC commitments is conditional</strong> upon governments receiving external support, highlighting the crucial role of regional cooperation in providing the technical assistance needed to turn these commitments into action.</li>
</ul>
<img src="/images/letters/261106_climate_ndc_report.jpeg" alt="Cover of the Ocean-based Climate Action in Asia and the Pacific report" width="250" style="display:block;width:250px;max-width:100%;height:auto;margin:0 auto;"/>
<p>For a more <strong>sector-oriented focus</strong>, <a href="https://www.unescap.org/kp?f%5B0%5D=kp_programme_of_work_facet%3A174">four policy briefs</a> were also released during Ocean Week highlighting key challenges and enablers for offshore wind energy in South-East Asia, blue carbon, green shipping and ocean-climate resilience in Asia and the Pacific.</p>

<hr/>
<p><strong>🔵 Other blue milestones at Ocean Week</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>The Agreement on Biodiversity Beyond National Jurisdictions caught participants' attention, with many sessions and discussions focusing on the Clearing-house mechanism, the Scientific and technical body and the needs of States in the region to implement the Treaty.</li>
<li>Taking place almost halfway between the third United Nations Ocean Conference 3 (UNOC3) in Nice, France and the fourth (UNOC4), which will be hosted by the Republic of Korea in 2028, Ocean Week served as a first milestone to take stock of the implementation of voluntary commitments and prepare the next conference.</li>
<li><a href="https://www.unep.org/cobsea/events/conference/sea-solutions-2026">The Sea of Solutions Forum</a>, organised during the week, provided an opportunity to discuss how to accelerate the implementation of effective solutions to plastic pollution.</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>💙 Ocean optimism</strong><br/>Asia-Pacific Ocean Week also marked the kick-off of a 30-month project funded by the Kunming Biodiversity Fund, aiming to support the establishment of new marine protected areas, while improving the management and connectivity of existing ones in Cambodia, the Maldives and the Philippines. The project will be led by <a href="https://www.unescap.org">ESCAP</a> and <a href="https://www.unep.org/cobsea/events/conference/sea-solutions-2026">UNEP-COBSEA</a>. The time for implementation has started!</p>
