<hr/><span style="font-weight: bolder;">Ocean update</span><br/>
According to a new paper, published in <a href="https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-020-2146-7"><em>Nature</em></a> this week, "substantial recovery of the abundance, structure and function of marine life could be achieved by 2050, if major pressures—including climate change—are mitigated. Rebuilding marine life represents a doable Grand Challenge for humanity, an ethical obligation and a smart economic objective to achieve a sustainable future." (not open access :( email me for pdf)<br/>
<img src="/images/letters/inferred-2020-04-03-1-capturedecran2020-04-03a172202.png" style="width: 640px; max-width: 640px;display:block;float:none;clear:both;height:auto;max-width:100%;margin:0.75rem auto;" width="640"/>The huge expanse of the high seas (waters that are beyond the jurisdiction of any State) remains unprotected. A <a href="https://www.pewtrusts.org/-/media/assets/2020/03/high_seas_mpa_report.pdf">new report by Pew Charitable Trusts</a> sets out a path to creating the first generation of high seas protected areas, using a science-based method to highlight 10 sites that would help safeguard biodiversity.<br/>
<img src="/images/letters/inferred-2020-04-03-2-capturedecran2020-04-03a171447.png" style="width: 522px; max-width: 522px;display:block;float:none;clear:both;height:auto;max-width:100%;margin:0.75rem auto;" width="522"/><img src="/images/letters/inferred-2020-04-03-3-capturedecran2020-04-03a171425.png" style="width: 374px; max-width: 374px;display:block;float:none;clear:both;height:auto;max-width:100%;margin:0.75rem auto;" width="374"/>Yet there is currently no legal framework to designate protected areas for international waters. The fourth and (supposedly, but probably not) final session of the <a href="https://www.iddri.org/en/publications-and-events/study/long-and-winding-road-negotiating-high-seas-treaty">high seas treaty negotiations</a> would have been wrapping up right now, so here's a few interesting reads:

<ul>
<li><a href="http://bit.ly/BBNJhighhopes" style="font-weight: 400; font-style: normal; font-size: 16px;">High Hopes for the High Seas</a><span style="font-size: 16px; font-style: normal; font-weight: 400;">: towards an ambitious treaty (IDDRI)</span></li>
<li>Seize the moment: towards <a href="https://pubs.iied.org/17479IIED/">fairer capacity building</a> and marine technology transfer (IIED)</li>
<li>Two of the largest protected areas on earth have had "little, if any, negative impacts on the fishing industry" (<a href="https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-020-14588-3.pdf">Nature</a>, open access)</li>
<li><a href="https://ocean.csis.org/commentary/threading-the-needle-balancing-conservation-and-security-in-the-high-seas-treaty/">Balancing conservation and security</a> in the high seas treaty (CSIS)</li>
</ul>
