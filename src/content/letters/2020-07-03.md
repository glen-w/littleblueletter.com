<hr style="font-weight: 400;"/><span style="font-weight: bolder; font-size: 16px;">Ocean update</span><br/>
<a href="https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/science-environment-53119686" style="font-size: 1em; font-weight: 400;">One-fifth of the seafloor has now been mapped</a><span style="font-size: 1em; font-weight: 400;">! </span>
<p dir="ltr" style="font-weight: 400; line-height: 1.38; margin-top: 0pt; margin-bottom: 0pt;"><span style="color: rgb(85, 85, 85); font-size: 16px; font-style: normal; font-weight: 400;">To celebrate World Hydrography Day, the Nippon Foundation-GEBCO <em>Seabed 2030 Project</em> announced that 19% of the world’s seafloor has now been mapped to modern standards. Seabed 2030 is an ambitious initiative to map 100% of the ocean floor by 2030 - very important for exploration and scientific advancement. This is an impressive feat as in 2017 only 6% of the seabed was mapped!</span><br/>

<div style="font-size: 16px;">
<hr style="font-weight: 400;"/><strong>Finding the Deep - Curator’s Story</strong>
<div>
<ul dir="ltr">
<li role="presentation">I’ve known I wanted to be a deep sea scientist since I was 18-years-old. The quote for this week’s newsletter is by Dr. Robert Ballard, whose <a href="https://www.ted.com/talks/robert_ballard_the_astonishing_hidden_world_of_the_deep_ocean?language=en">TED Talk</a> on the importance of ocean exploration inspired me to become a marine scientist.</li>
<li role="presentation">I was lucky enough to get to meet and sail with Dr. Ballard on the Exploration Vessel the E/V <a href="https://nautiluslive.org/">Nautilus</a> and sat next to him while we explored the Eastern Pacific with the remotely operated vehicle, Hercules. </li>
</ul>
<p dir="ltr" role="presentation"><img src="/images/letters/inferred-2020-07-03-3-kelseyonnautilus.jpg" style="width: 300px; max-width: 300px;display:block;float:none;clear:both;height:auto;max-width:100%;margin:0.75rem auto;" width="300"/></p>
<ul dir="ltr">
<li role="presentation">​The deep sea is waiting to be discovered, and while onboard we found and sampled what is likely a new species of velutinid snail!</li>
</ul>
<p dir="ltr" role="presentation"><img src="/images/letters/inferred-2020-07-03-4-profilepic.jpg" style="width: 300px; max-width: 300px;display:block;float:none;clear:both;height:auto;max-width:100%;margin:0.75rem auto;" width="300"/></p>
<p dir="ltr" role="presentation" style="text-align: center;"><em>A new species? (Ocean Exploration Trust)</em></p>
<ul dir="ltr">
<li role="presentation">For any aspiring ocean explorers out there, my advice is to follow your passion with perseverance and determination. I’m incredibly lucky to get to discover more about the deep sea every week, and I hope more students and researchers will be able to as well! </li>
</ul>
