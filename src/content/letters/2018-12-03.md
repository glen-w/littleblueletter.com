<div style="font-weight: 400; font-style: normal; font-size: 16px; color: rgb(85, 85, 85);"><strong>Ocean update</strong>
<ul>
<li>The <a href="https://cop24.gov.pl/" style="font-weight: 400; font-style: normal; font-size: 16px;">climate COP</a><span style="font-size: 16px; font-style: normal; font-weight: 400;"> is taking place right now in Katowice, Poland. The signatories and partners of the </span><a href="http://www.vardagroup.org/wp-content/uploads/2016/10/Because-the-Ocean-Peru.pdf" style="font-weight: 400; font-style: normal; font-size: 16px;">Because the Ocean declaration</a><span style="font-size: 16px; font-style: normal; font-weight: 400;"> are making sure that ocean issues are on the agenda (watch their event </span><a href="https://vimeo.com/305044214" style="font-weight: 400; font-style: normal; font-size: 16px;">here</a><span style="font-size: 16px; font-style: normal; font-weight: 400;">).</span></li>
<li><a href="https://en.unesco.org/ocean-decade/epg">The UN Decade of Ocean Science</a> is set to begin on January 2021: <a href="https://en.unesco.org/ocean-decade/epg">meet the team</a> of experts helping to prepare.</li>
<li>The <a href="https://ourocean2018.org/">Our Ocean conference</a> took place in Bali at the end of October, with over 300 wide-ranging <a href="https://ourocean2018.org/?l=our-ocean-commitments">commitments</a> adding 14 million km2 of new protected areas and unlocking <span style="color: rgb(85, 85, 85); font-size: 16px; font-style: normal; font-weight: 400;">$10,7 billion of new financing.</span></li>
<li><span style="font-weight: 400; font-style: normal; font-size: 16px; color: rgb(85, 85, 85);">Plastic needs replacing and </span><a href="http://www.parley.tv/updates/2018/10/24/parley-x-biofabricate-2018" style="font-weight: 400; font-style: normal; font-size: 16px;">Biofabricate</a><span style="color: rgb(85, 85, 85); font-size: 16px; font-style: normal; font-weight: 400;"><a href="http://www.parley.tv/updates/2018/10/24/parley-x-biofabricate-2018"> 2018</a> is showcasing the materials of the future.</span></li>
<li><span style="color: rgb(85, 85, 85); font-size: 16px; font-style: normal; font-weight: 400;">The Intergovernmental Oceanographic Commission and the European Commission have launched MSPglobal, </span><a href="http://www.unesco.org/new/en/natural-sciences/ioc-oceans/single-view-oceans/news/maritime_spatial_planning_goes_global_ioc_unesco_and_europe/" style="font-weight: 400; font-style: normal; font-size: 16px;">a new initiative</a><span style="color: rgb(85, 85, 85); font-size: 16px; font-style: normal; font-weight: 400;"> to promote cross-border maritime spatial planning.</span></li>
</ul>

<hr style="color: rgb(85, 85, 85);"/><span style="font-weight: bolder; color: rgb(85, 85, 85); font-size: 16px; font-style: normal;">Cod-bothering  </span><br style="color: rgb(85, 85, 85);"/>
<span style="color: rgb(85, 85, 85); font-size: 16px; font-style: normal; font-weight: 400;">The growing number of ships traversing the western Canadian Arctic is generating noise that </span><a href="https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2018/11/181105122452.htm" style="font-weight: 400; font-style: normal; font-size: 16px;">can affect marine animals</a><span style="color: rgb(85, 85, 85); font-size: 16px; font-style: normal; font-weight: 400;">. Shipping</span><span style="color: rgb(85, 85, 85); font-size: 16px; font-style: normal; font-weight: 400;"> traffic can drown out other sounds, reducing the ability of cod and other marine animals to communicate, forage, avoid predators, reproduce, and navigate.</span></div>

<strong>Endangered Species Act is Endangered </strong><br/>
The <span style="font-size: 16px; font-style: normal; font-weight: 400;">US' Endangered Species Act, a crucial piece of environmental protection legislation, may itself be facing extinction. The Supreme Court recently <a href="http://www.sfexaminer.com/supreme-court-limits-habitats-protected-endangered-species-act/">restricted the scope</a> of the law (in an unusual case regarding a particularly rare frog) and Congress is cutting funding. Ocean is <a href="http://act.oceana.org/page/20755/action/1?ea.tracking.id=twitter&amp;utm_campaign=Advo&amp;utm_content=20181109ESATweet&amp;utm_source=Twitter&amp;utm_medium=Social&amp;utm_id=yVk8WzC0dV6lud">fighting back</a>.</span>
