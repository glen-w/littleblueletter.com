<div style="font-weight: 400; font-style: normal; font-size: 16px; color: rgb(85, 85, 85);"><strong>The Ocean is for Life, not just a Day</strong>
<div style="font-weight: 400; font-style: normal; font-size: 16px; color: rgb(85, 85, 85);">Clean-ups and other inspiring ocean events were organized around the world yesterday to mark <a href="https://unworldoceansday.org/" style="font-size: 16px;">World Oceans Day</a>. T<span style="font-weight: 400; font-style: normal; font-size: 16px;">housands in the US took part in the </span><a href="https://twitter.com/hashtag/MarchForTheOcean?src=hash" style="font-weight: 400; font-style: normal; font-size: 16px;">#MarchForTheOcean</a><span style="font-weight: 400; font-style: normal; font-size: 16px;"> - </span><em style="font-weight: 400; font-size: 16px;">“Our seas are rising, and so are we!” - </em><span style="font-weight: 400; font-style: normal; font-size: 16px;">while flotilla of boats and kayaks paddled down the polluted Anacostia River.</span></div>
<div style="font-weight: 400; font-style: normal; font-size: 16px; color: rgb(85, 85, 85);"><img src="/images/letters/inferred-2018-06-08-1-ezgif-2-764aaac643.gif" style="font-size: 16px; outline-color: rgb(248, 174, 176); width: 452px; max-width: 452px;display:block;float:none;clear:both;height:auto;max-width:100%;margin:0.75rem auto;" width="452"/></div>
World Environment Day was also this week, with hosts India picking the theme,  “Beat Plastic Pollution” . The Indian Government recently stepped up action to combat its plastic problem, banning all forms of disposable plastics in its capital, Delhi, and pledging to <a href="http://worldenvironmentday.global/en/news/india-pledges-clean-100-historic-sites">clean up 100 of its historic sites</a>, including the famous Taj Mahal. <br/>
<br/>
The Geneva office of the UN marked the occasion by announcing that its on-site catering service will ditch single-use plastic takeaway containers and cutlery. An exhibition in the building highlighted Lake Geneva's pollution problem with some beautiful artwork made from marine plastic:<br/>

<hr/><span style="font-weight: bolder; font-size: 16px;">#OceanOptimism</span>
<ul>
<li>A small fishing community in Kenya is <a href="https://www.newsdeeply.com/oceans/articles/2018/05/31/meet-the-small-fishing-community-regrowing-coral-reefs-in-kenya">regrowing coral reefs</a> - between 2013 and 2017 the community planted 1,225 individual corals in a 3 square km area.</li>
<li>Chile just became the first South American country to ban single-use plastic bags.</li>
<li>The <a href="https://onelessstraw.org/">OneLessStraw Campaign</a> had a big win this week when Hilton Hotels announced that it will remove plastic straws from its 650 hotels - 35 million straws per year!</li>
</ul>

<div style="text-align: left;"><strong>From the editor</strong><br/>
No idea what “gravity effects” are? Me neither. A scientist on the Longest Swim research projects <a href="http://thelongestswim.com/gravity-effect-intvw/">says</a>: <em>“Land-based research into the effects of low gravity can only eliminate one gravitational gradient: head-to-foot. Ben’s immersion in water will remove a second gradient, the front-to-back, making it a more ideal analogue to space travel.”</em> Basically, he will barely stand up for six months, which is kinda strange and sort of like floating in space. The inimitable Sylvia Earle just did an <a href="https://old.reddit.com/r/NatureIsFuckingLit/comments/8pjc9x/im_dr_sylvia_earle_oceanographer_and_explorer_and/">“Ask Me Anything”</a> on Reddit. I've logged ~50 hours underwater, and I've been astounded by what I've seen. Sylvia Earle has logged 7,000 hours. When she talks about the ocean, we should all listen very carefully.</div>
