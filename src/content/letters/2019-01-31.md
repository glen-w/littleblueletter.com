<p style="font-weight: 400; font-style: normal; font-size: 16px; color: rgb(85, 85, 85); text-align: center;"><em>"You must not lose faith in humanity.<br/>
Humanity is like an ocean; if a few drops of the ocean are dirty, the ocean does not become dirty."</em><br/>
<span style="font-weight: 400; font-style: normal; font-size: 16px;">~ </span>Mahatma Gandhi</p>
<div style="font-weight: 400; font-style: normal; font-size: 16px; color: rgb(85, 85, 85);">
<div style="font-size: 16px;">
<hr/><span style="font-weight: bolder;">Creating a generation of change through pop culture &amp; social media</span></div>
<div style="font-size: 16px;">
<div style="font-weight: 400; font-style: normal; font-size: 16px;"><span style="font-weight: 400; font-style: normal; font-size: 16px;">At Ocean Generation, we are trying to understand the vast networks of habits, passions, beliefs and politics, that guide our</span><span style="font-weight: 400; font-style: normal; font-size: 16px;"> </span><span style="font-weight: 400; font-style: normal; font-size: 16px;">behaviour</span><span style="font-weight: 400; font-style: normal; font-size: 16px;">. </span><span style="font-weight: 400; font-style: normal; font-size: 16px;">Popular culture - t</span><span style="font-weight: 400; font-style: normal; font-size: 16px;">he books we do or don’t read, the clothes we buy, the food we eat, the media we consume both consciously and subconsciously - is a pervasive</span><span style="font-weight: 400; font-style: normal; font-size: 16px;"> influence on our lives and behaviour. We want </span><span style="font-weight: 400; font-style: normal; font-size: 16px;">to leverage this influence </span><span style="font-weight: 400; font-style: normal; font-size: 16px;">to shift habits and inspire political change</span><span style="font-weight: 400; font-style: normal; font-size: 16px;">. </span><br/>
<img src="/images/letters/inferred-2019-01-31-1-ezgif-2-39086ccb83b6.gif" style="width: 350px; max-width: 350px;display:block;float:none;clear:both;height:auto;max-width:100%;margin:0.75rem auto;" width="350"/>With the explosion of social media, pop culture can spreads far and wide with unprecedented speed. The impact of such tools are yet to be fully known, but we see them disrupting politics, economies and democracies around the world. Imagine the impact if we could direct this power towards promoting behavioural change for the ocean. </div>
</div>
<div style="font-size: 16px;"><br/>
So what are we doing? Helping 14,000,000 monthly players of the <em style="font-size: 16px;">Hungry Shark</em> game to “<a href="http://oceangeneration.org/project/seaitdifferently/" style="font-size: 16px;">Sea It Differently</a>” Getting popular YouTube musicians to <a href="http://oceangeneration.org/project/the-last-straw/" style="font-size: 16px;">sing for the ocean</a>. A <a href="http://oceangeneration.org/news/dadlihack-2019/" style="font-size: 16px;">hackathon</a> to develop Infrastructure and resilience to climate change. A movement to <a href="http://oceangeneration.org/project/the-last-straw/" style="font-size: 16px;">get rid of straws</a>.</div>
