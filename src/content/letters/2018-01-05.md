<hr/><strong>What to watch in 2018</strong><br/>
Oceans Deeply has published their <a href="https://www.newsdeeply.com/oceans/articles/2018/01/02/in-2018-keep-an-eye-on-these-big-ocean-issues-and-trends">outlook for 2018</a>: negotiations for a new high seas treaty, electric and autonomous container ships, a Paris Agreement for Shipping, sustainable aquaculture, and a <a href="https://www.marchforocean.com">March for the Ocean</a>. Our review of 2017 suggests that ocean plastic and marine litter, marine protected areas, and climate change will also be hot topics.

<div style="font-weight: normal; font-style: normal; font-size: 16px; color: rgb(85, 85, 85);">Along with 17 million others, I ended my 2017 watching in awe as Sir David Attenborough and the <span class="il">Blue</span> Planet II team showed us spellbinding images of an underwater world “beyond our imagination". <br/>
<br/>
			I chuckled as <a data-saferedirecturl="https://www.google.com/url?hl=en-GB&amp;q=http://mail01.tinyletterapp.com/LittleBlueLetter/little-blue-letter-s-review-of-2017-1/10383681-www.youtube.com/watch?c%3De22b91e7-3ad9-4b15-ac4f-fef8c0d79ffe&amp;source=gmail&amp;ust=1515255059126000&amp;usg=AFQjCNHEBAIzFR-72qodNH-BRSOfwge1IQ" href="http://mail01.tinyletterapp.com/LittleBlueLetter/little-blue-letter-s-review-of-2017-1/10383681-www.youtube.com/watch?c=e22b91e7-3ad9-4b15-ac4f-fef8c0d79ffe" style="color: rgb(52, 102, 204); text-decoration-line: none;" target="_blank">king penguins</a> waddled their way through hordes of high-strung elephant seals, and stared with my mouth agape as a giant trevally <a data-saferedirecturl="https://www.google.com/url?hl=en-GB&amp;q=http://mail01.tinyletterapp.com/LittleBlueLetter/little-blue-letter-s-review-of-2017-1/10383685-www.youtube.com/watch?c%3De22b91e7-3ad9-4b15-ac4f-fef8c0d79ffe&amp;source=gmail&amp;ust=1515255059126000&amp;usg=AFQjCNE9YdUSiN-WxphbxaZI_bpHBZ5dIw" href="http://mail01.tinyletterapp.com/LittleBlueLetter/little-blue-letter-s-review-of-2017-1/10383685-www.youtube.com/watch?c=e22b91e7-3ad9-4b15-ac4f-fef8c0d79ffe" style="color: rgb(52, 102, 204); text-decoration-line: none;" target="_blank">launched itself from the water</a> to swallow a fledgling tern whole. The series brought the wonders of the ocean into our living rooms, capturing the public imagination as never before.<br/>
<br/>
			I nonetheless worried that the show painted too rosy a picture of an ocean we know to be in grave danger. There were hints in each episode. I felt a pang of deep sadness as a mothering seal struggled to find a safe space for her pup amidst the retreating sea ice. I sobbed as a pilot whale refused to part with her dead calf even days after its death. <br/>
<br/>
			In the finale, Sir David did not hold back as he raised the alarm:
			<blockquote style="border-left: 1px solid rgb(221, 221, 221); margin: 20px 0px 20px 20px; padding-left: 20px; width: 544px; font-size: 16.8px; font-weight: normal; word-wrap: break-word; color: rgb(144, 144, 144);">
<p style="margin-top: 1em; margin-bottom: 20px; font-size: 16px; font-weight: normal; word-wrap: break-word; font-style: italic; line-height: 24px;">It is now clear our actions are having a significant impact on the world’s oceans. [They] are under threat now as never before in human history.</p>
</blockquote>
<span class="il">Blue</span> Planet II was an extraordinary ode to the beauty of our ocean and a stark reminder of the challenges that lie ahead. The series was also a fitting end to a year of unprecedented action for the ocean - if 2017 was the year the ocean entered into the public consciousness, it might also be remembered as the moment the tide started to turn.<br/>

			The <a data-saferedirecturl="https://www.google.com/url?hl=en-GB&amp;q=http://mail01.tinyletterapp.com/LittleBlueLetter/little-blue-letter-s-review-of-2017-1/10383849-www.ccamlr.org/en/news/2016/ccamlr-create-worlds-largest-marine-protected-area?c%3De22b91e7-3ad9-4b15-ac4f-fef8c0d79ffe&amp;source=gmail&amp;ust=1515255059129000&amp;usg=AFQjCNFozQ4Zos-pkuSTZemM8pvGOnzhow" href="http://mail01.tinyletterapp.com/LittleBlueLetter/little-blue-letter-s-review-of-2017-1/10383849-www.ccamlr.org/en/news/2016/ccamlr-create-worlds-largest-marine-protected-area?c=e22b91e7-3ad9-4b15-ac4f-fef8c0d79ffe" style="color: rgb(52, 102, 204); text-decoration-line: none;" target="_blank">Ross Sea marine protected area</a> (MPA) just came into force, having been agreed upon following intense diplomatic discussions at the meeting of the Convention on the Conservation of Antarctic Marine Living Resources (CCAMLR) last year. Unfortunately, States failed to maintain the momentum at this year's meeting, as negotiations for further MPAs <a data-saferedirecturl="https://www.google.com/url?hl=en-GB&amp;q=http://mail01.tinyletterapp.com/LittleBlueLetter/little-blue-letter-s-review-of-2017-1/10383853-www.antarcticocean.org/2017/10/antarctic-marine-protection-torpedoed/?c%3De22b91e7-3ad9-4b15-ac4f-fef8c0d79ffe&amp;source=gmail&amp;ust=1515255059129000&amp;usg=AFQjCNEChg38o-2XYouaglxBSHW1-vC8yQ" href="http://mail01.tinyletterapp.com/LittleBlueLetter/little-blue-letter-s-review-of-2017-1/10383853-www.antarcticocean.org/2017/10/antarctic-marine-protection-torpedoed/?c=e22b91e7-3ad9-4b15-ac4f-fef8c0d79ffe" style="color: rgb(52, 102, 204); text-decoration-line: none;" target="_blank">fell apart</a>.<br/>

<hr/><strong>Bouncing back?</strong><br/>
			Conservation efforts seem to be helping <a data-saferedirecturl="https://www.google.com/url?hl=en-GB&amp;q=http://mail01.tinyletterapp.com/LittleBlueLetter/little-blue-letter-s-review-of-2017-1/10383857-www.nytimes.com/2017/09/20/science/sea-turtles-conservation.html?c%3De22b91e7-3ad9-4b15-ac4f-fef8c0d79ffe&amp;source=gmail&amp;ust=1515255059129000&amp;usg=AFQjCNG7vG6Wn-53HjoiFuPTDEKV4FSFbw" href="http://mail01.tinyletterapp.com/LittleBlueLetter/little-blue-letter-s-review-of-2017-1/10383857-www.nytimes.com/2017/09/20/science/sea-turtles-conservation.html?c=e22b91e7-3ad9-4b15-ac4f-fef8c0d79ffe" style="color: rgb(52, 102, 204); text-decoration-line: none;" target="_blank">sea turtles</a>, with a study suggesting that they are bouncing back. Others, like the highly endangered <a data-saferedirecturl="https://www.google.com/url?hl=en-GB&amp;q=http://mail01.tinyletterapp.com/LittleBlueLetter/little-blue-letter-s-review-of-2017-1/10383861-www.theguardian.com/environment/2017/may/16/chinese-appetite-totoaba-fish-bladder-threatens-rare-vaquita?c%3De22b91e7-3ad9-4b15-ac4f-fef8c0d79ffe&amp;source=gmail&amp;ust=1515255059129000&amp;usg=AFQjCNEi4ascydOfG3bdGzDATnzWdLwrXw" href="http://mail01.tinyletterapp.com/LittleBlueLetter/little-blue-letter-s-review-of-2017-1/10383861-www.theguardian.com/environment/2017/may/16/chinese-appetite-totoaba-fish-bladder-threatens-rare-vaquita?c=e22b91e7-3ad9-4b15-ac4f-fef8c0d79ffe" style="color: rgb(52, 102, 204); text-decoration-line: none;" target="_blank">Vaquita</a>, have not been so lucky - there are now just 30 left in the wild.<br/>
			 <br/>
			Members of the International Commission for the Conservation of Atlantic Tunas (ICCAT) decided to increase the catch quota for Bluefin tuna, going against scientific advice and putting long-term recovery of the stock <a data-saferedirecturl="https://www.google.com/url?hl=en-GB&amp;q=http://mail01.tinyletterapp.com/LittleBlueLetter/little-blue-letter-s-review-of-2017-1/10383865-www.euractiv.com/section/energy-environment/opinion/disgraceful-behaviour-by-the-eu-at-high-level-tuna-meeting-in-marrakesh/?c%3De22b91e7-3ad9-4b15-ac4f-fef8c0d79ffe&amp;source=gmail&amp;ust=1515255059129000&amp;usg=AFQjCNHWucIULjAuCBQ8WAFI6iSwlmglnA" href="http://mail01.tinyletterapp.com/LittleBlueLetter/little-blue-letter-s-review-of-2017-1/10383865-www.euractiv.com/section/energy-environment/opinion/disgraceful-behaviour-by-the-eu-at-high-level-tuna-meeting-in-marrakesh/?c=e22b91e7-3ad9-4b15-ac4f-fef8c0d79ffe" style="color: rgb(52, 102, 204); text-decoration-line: none;" target="_blank">at risk</a>. The news from the meeting of the <a data-saferedirecturl="https://www.google.com/url?hl=en-GB&amp;q=http://mail01.tinyletterapp.com/LittleBlueLetter/little-blue-letter-s-review-of-2017-1/10383869-www.cms.int/sites/default/files/cms_cop12_pr_closing_e_0.pdf?c%3De22b91e7-3ad9-4b15-ac4f-fef8c0d79ffe&amp;source=gmail&amp;ust=1515255059129000&amp;usg=AFQjCNEhWCbuuSPTxFSTKqFwsr8z5gStUg" href="http://mail01.tinyletterapp.com/LittleBlueLetter/little-blue-letter-s-review-of-2017-1/10383869-www.cms.int/sites/default/files/cms_cop12_pr_closing_e_0.pdf?c=e22b91e7-3ad9-4b15-ac4f-fef8c0d79ffe" style="color: rgb(52, 102, 204); text-decoration-line: none;" target="_blank">Convention on Migratory Species</a> was more positive, with Parties moving to protect six shark and ray species.<br/>

<hr/><strong>Look to the (<span class="il">blue</span>) future</strong><br/>
			Even as the ocean faces immense pressures from all angles, humans continue to look to the '<span class="il">Blue</span> Economy' for economic growth. Many countries are heavily investing in fish farming and tourism, while offshore wind has <a data-saferedirecturl="https://www.google.com/url?hl=en-GB&amp;q=http://mail01.tinyletterapp.com/LittleBlueLetter/little-blue-letter-s-review-of-2017-1/10383873-www.nytimes.com/2017/02/07/business/energy-environment/renewables-offshore-wind-green-power-dong.html?c%3De22b91e7-3ad9-4b15-ac4f-fef8c0d79ffe&amp;source=gmail&amp;ust=1515255059129000&amp;usg=AFQjCNF_XsIZ8M1ybqS6suHzJmxrredNug" href="http://mail01.tinyletterapp.com/LittleBlueLetter/little-blue-letter-s-review-of-2017-1/10383873-www.nytimes.com/2017/02/07/business/energy-environment/renewables-offshore-wind-green-power-dong.html?c=e22b91e7-3ad9-4b15-ac4f-fef8c0d79ffe" style="color: rgb(52, 102, 204); text-decoration-line: none;" target="_blank">moved into the mainstream</a>. Development of wave and tidal energy requires careful consideration if it is to provide energy without harming the environment, while seabed mining inches ever closer to fruition, despite the <a data-saferedirecturl="https://www.google.com/url?hl=en-GB&amp;q=http://mail01.tinyletterapp.com/LittleBlueLetter/little-blue-letter-s-review-of-2017-1/10383877-science.sciencemag.org/content/349/6244/144?c%3De22b91e7-3ad9-4b15-ac4f-fef8c0d79ffe&amp;source=gmail&amp;ust=1515255059129000&amp;usg=AFQjCNGjJ5vAy1ywzwfmmIML-PDrMQwo9Q" href="http://mail01.tinyletterapp.com/LittleBlueLetter/little-blue-letter-s-review-of-2017-1/10383877-science.sciencemag.org/content/349/6244/144?c=e22b91e7-3ad9-4b15-ac4f-fef8c0d79ffe" style="color: rgb(52, 102, 204); text-decoration-line: none;" target="_blank">risks and scientific uncertainty</a>. <br/>
			 <br/>
			Microsoft has even produced a prototype of an <a data-saferedirecturl="https://www.google.com/url?hl=en-GB&amp;q=http://mail01.tinyletterapp.com/LittleBlueLetter/little-blue-letter-s-review-of-2017-1/10383881-www.nytimes.com/2016/02/01/technology/microsoft-plumbs-oceans-depths-to-test-underwater-data-center.html?c%3De22b91e7-3ad9-4b15-ac4f-fef8c0d79ffe&amp;source=gmail&amp;ust=1515255059129000&amp;usg=AFQjCNGsm8xQIAz8LzG6JPhhYv5ub151nw" href="http://mail01.tinyletterapp.com/LittleBlueLetter/little-blue-letter-s-review-of-2017-1/10383881-www.nytimes.com/2016/02/01/technology/microsoft-plumbs-oceans-depths-to-test-underwater-data-center.html?c=e22b91e7-3ad9-4b15-ac4f-fef8c0d79ffe" style="color: rgb(52, 102, 204); text-decoration-line: none;" target="_blank">underwater data centre</a> that uses the sea to cool energy intensive servers. (Google has already <a data-saferedirecturl="https://www.google.com/url?hl=en-GB&amp;q=http://mail01.tinyletterapp.com/LittleBlueLetter/little-blue-letter-s-review-of-2017-1/10383885-www.google.com/patents/us7525207?c%3De22b91e7-3ad9-4b15-ac4f-fef8c0d79ffe&amp;source=gmail&amp;ust=1515255059129000&amp;usg=AFQjCNFZ2FdC47rm6xSSolcSrN5nPID_KA" href="http://mail01.tinyletterapp.com/LittleBlueLetter/little-blue-letter-s-review-of-2017-1/10383885-www.google.com/patents/us7525207?c=e22b91e7-3ad9-4b15-ac4f-fef8c0d79ffe" style="color: rgb(52, 102, 204); text-decoration-line: none;" target="_blank">patented</a> a similar system, powered by wave energy).

			<hr/><strong>Conserve and protect</strong><br/>
			The trend for declaring huge MPAs continued in 2017. Chile declared an MPA almost as big as the country itself around <a data-saferedirecturl="https://www.google.com/url?hl=en-GB&amp;q=http://mail01.tinyletterapp.com/LittleBlueLetter/little-blue-letter-s-review-of-2017-1/10383889-www.theguardian.com/environment/2017/sep/09/easter-island-marine-park-save-endemic-species-indigenous-lives?c%3De22b91e7-3ad9-4b15-ac4f-fef8c0d79ffe&amp;source=gmail&amp;ust=1515255059129000&amp;usg=AFQjCNE8Esfh9COwM_J2-73czHATGx6IXw" href="http://mail01.tinyletterapp.com/LittleBlueLetter/little-blue-letter-s-review-of-2017-1/10383889-www.theguardian.com/environment/2017/sep/09/easter-island-marine-park-save-endemic-species-indigenous-lives?c=e22b91e7-3ad9-4b15-ac4f-fef8c0d79ffe" style="color: rgb(52, 102, 204); text-decoration-line: none;" target="_blank">Easter Island</a>, banning extractive industries and industrial fishing. Mexico put an area the size of Illinois off limits for fishing and other extractive activities, <a data-saferedirecturl="https://www.google.com/url?hl=en-GB&amp;q=http://mail01.tinyletterapp.com/LittleBlueLetter/little-blue-letter-s-review-of-2017-1/10383893-www.newsdeeply.com/oceans/articles/2017/12/06/why-mexicos-new-marine-reserve-is-a-model-for-ocean-protection?c%3De22b91e7-3ad9-4b15-ac4f-fef8c0d79ffe&amp;source=gmail&amp;ust=1515255059129000&amp;usg=AFQjCNH_8nuqa5xMTGLJB4z8a0kMfOiZKw" href="http://mail01.tinyletterapp.com/LittleBlueLetter/little-blue-letter-s-review-of-2017-1/10383893-www.newsdeeply.com/oceans/articles/2017/12/06/why-mexicos-new-marine-reserve-is-a-model-for-ocean-protection?c=e22b91e7-3ad9-4b15-ac4f-fef8c0d79ffe" style="color: rgb(52, 102, 204); text-decoration-line: none;" target="_blank">protecting</a> the pristine and productive <span style="font-size: 16px; font-style: normal; font-weight: normal;">shark-infested waters</span><span style="font-size: 16px; font-style: normal; font-weight: normal;"> around a small group of islands 400km off the country's Pacific coast (an abundance of sharks is a </span><a data-saferedirecturl="https://www.google.com/url?hl=en-GB&amp;q=http://mail01.tinyletterapp.com/LittleBlueLetter/little-blue-letter-s-review-of-2017-1/10383897-e360.yale.edu/features/the_crucial_role_of_predators_a_new_perspective_on_ecology?c%3De22b91e7-3ad9-4b15-ac4f-fef8c0d79ffe&amp;source=gmail&amp;ust=1515255059130000&amp;usg=AFQjCNHb2XFEz4kwjoMbyn1e7mUurobFXg" href="http://mail01.tinyletterapp.com/LittleBlueLetter/little-blue-letter-s-review-of-2017-1/10383897-e360.yale.edu/features/the_crucial_role_of_predators_a_new_perspective_on_ecology?c=e22b91e7-3ad9-4b15-ac4f-fef8c0d79ffe" style="color: rgb(52, 102, 204); text-decoration-line: none; font-size: 16px; font-style: normal; font-weight: normal;" target="_blank">good thing</a><span style="font-size: 16px; font-style: normal; font-weight: normal;">, honestly).</span></div>
<div style="font-style: normal; font-size: 16px; color: rgb(85, 85, 85);"><br/>
			The island I call home is relatively small, so it is hard to believe that the UK is custodian of the fifth largest marine estate in the world. Yet the vast waters around our remote Overseas Territories are home to the planet's largest coral atoll and as much as a quarter of its penguins. Conservationists are now <a data-saferedirecturl="https://www.google.com/url?hl=en-GB&amp;q=http://mail01.tinyletterapp.com/LittleBlueLetter/little-blue-letter-s-review-of-2017-1/10383901-greatbritishoceans.org/?c%3De22b91e7-3ad9-4b15-ac4f-fef8c0d79ffe&amp;source=gmail&amp;ust=1515255059130000&amp;usg=AFQjCNHvOM7SF1hz4GvEu5rVpGJWAfz0Qg" href="http://mail01.tinyletterapp.com/LittleBlueLetter/little-blue-letter-s-review-of-2017-1/10383901-greatbritishoceans.org/?c=e22b91e7-3ad9-4b15-ac4f-fef8c0d79ffe" style="color: rgb(52, 102, 204); text-decoration-line: none; font-weight: normal;" target="_blank">lobbying the government</a> to create a “<span class="il">blue</span> belt” around these islands, allowing ocean ecosystems to recover and marine life to thrive.<br/>

<hr/><b>It's only just begun...</b><br/>
			While it is possible to find glimmers of hope in this time of unprecedented crisis, our life support system and its many inhabitants are going to need far more help if they are to survive and thrive. If 2017 was the moment the tide started to turn, 2018 must be the year we take action. An ocean in crisis needs more than "paper parks" and promises.</div>
