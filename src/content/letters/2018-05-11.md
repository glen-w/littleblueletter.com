<div style="font-weight: 400; font-style: normal; font-size: 16px; color: rgb(85, 85, 85);"><strong>Grand plans in Vietnam </strong> <br/>
Vietnam has been working for over a decade on the development of a coastal Marine Protected Area (MPA) network. <a href="https://www.iucn.org/content/improving-management-vietnams-marine-protected-area-network-first-steps">Nine MPAs now dot the coastline</a>, home to coral reefs, endangered sea turtles, and seagrass communities that act as important carbon sinks. <span style="color: rgb(85, 85, 85); font-size: 16px; font-style: normal; font-weight: 400;">The Cham Islands MPA, </span><span style="font-weight: 400; font-style: normal; font-size: 16px; color: rgb(85, 85, 85);">off the coast of tourist-friendly Hoi An City,</span><span style="color: rgb(85, 85, 85); font-size: 16px; font-style: normal; font-weight: 400;"> is one of the network's flagship sites </span><span style="font-weight: 400; font-style: normal; font-size: 16px; color: rgb(85, 85, 85);">and a UNESCO-designated biosphere reserve</span><span style="color: rgb(85, 85, 85); font-size: 16px; font-style: normal; font-weight: 400;">. </span>
<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="/images/letters/8aba33f5-7bc6-4510-a8d7-ef5744d39005.png" style="width: 376px; max-width: 376px;display:block;float:none;clear:both;height:auto;max-width:100%;margin:0.75rem auto;" width="376"/></div>
With fishing restrictions in place, a key challenge has been to help this island community of 3,000 people to transition from a traditional fishing economy to tourism. <span style="color: rgb(85, 85, 85); font-size: 16px; font-style: normal; font-weight: 400;">Marine biologist Dr. Chu Manh Trinh has provided this support, engaging and negotiating with the community to ensure that the MPA works for everyone</span>.<br/>
<img src="/images/letters/e3e7d4e7-944f-4048-b511-15cb2303ed2d.png" style="width: 376px; max-width: 376px;display:block;float:none;clear:both;height:auto;max-width:100%;margin:0.75rem auto;" width="376"/><span style="color: rgb(85, 85, 85); font-size: 16px; font-style: normal; font-weight: 400;">Trinh is now sharing lessons learned from the Cham Islands MPA with Vietnam's newest MPA, the Ly Son islands. The Ly Son MPA management plan includes innovative measures requiring the only tour operator to spend part of its revenue on conservation and on the local community.</span><br/>
<br/>
But too much tourism could bring problems like reef trampling and overwhelming waste (four million tourists visited the Cham Islands in 2017—quite a large number for a small MPA). Trinh and his colleagues have pioneered measures such as the nation’s first plastic bag ban in order to address these challenges.
<hr/><strong>#OceanOptimism</strong><br/>
Most marine litter comes from <a href="https://www.pri.org/stories/2016-01-13/5-countries-dump-more-plastic-oceans-rest-world-combined">a relatively small geographic area</a> - in fast-developing countries where the demand for consumer products has outpaced development of waste-management infrastructure. Fortunately, simple improvements to garbage collection and storage can prevent leaks and drastically reduce litter. Local leaders in Vietnam are fighting ocean plastic by <a href="https://www.pacificenvironment.org/trash-talk-community-leaders-fighting-ocean-plastic-trash-vietnam/">building a zero waste coalition</a>.<br/>
