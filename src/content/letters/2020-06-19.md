<hr style="font-weight: 400;"/><span style="font-weight: bolder; font-size: 16px;">Will the pandemic fast-track seabed mining?</span>
<ul>
<li style="font-weight: 400; font-size: 16px;">The ongoing coronavirus pandemic has <a href="https://chinadialogueocean.net/13685-covid-19-could-throw-seabed-mining-negotiations-off-track/">disrupted negotiations for developing seabed mining regulations</a></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400; font-size: 16px;">One company, <em>Deep Green</em>, says it is considering invoking a rule that would compel approval of mining plans within 2 years.</li>
<li style="font-weight: 400; font-size: 16px;">The head of the International Seabed Authority (ISA), the body responsible for passing a mining code, has been <a href="https://twitter.com/SeasAtRisk/status/1275813220868075522?s=20">surprisingly vocal</a> in his opposition to a moratorium on seabed mining, saying that it would be 'anti-science, anti-knowledge, anti-development, and anti-international law'.</li>
<li style="font-weight: 400; font-size: 16px;">"It is crucial to ensure that the current crisis is not used as an excuse to ‘fast-track’ any decision-making by curtailing the prevailing norms of due process, inclusivity and good governance”, says Duncan Currie, international lawyer and member of the Deep Sea Conservation Coalition</li>
<li style="font-weight: 400; font-size: 16px;">The Secretary-General of the ISA is a <a href="https://twitter.com/mwlodge/" style="font-weight: 400;">self-described</a> "advocate for rule of law in the ocean", but his independence has <a href="http://www.deepseaminingoutofourdepth.org/wp-content/uploads/Why-the-Rush.pdf" style="font-weight: 400;">been called into question</a>.</li>
</ul>
