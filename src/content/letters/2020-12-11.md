<hr/><span style="font-weight: bolder; font-size: 16px;">A New Ocean Agenda</span>
<div style="font-size: 16px;">
<div style="font-size: 16px;">The <em style="font-size: 16px;">High Level Panel for Sustainable Ocean Economy</em> has launched an ambitious <a href="https://oceanpanel.org/" style="font-size: 16px;">call to action</a>, with the leaders of 14 countries committing to sustainably manage 100% of their waters by 2025 and to support global ocean protection targets. <span style="font-size: 16px; font-style: normal; font-weight: 400;">The announcement is a "</span><a href="https://www.nationalgeographic.com/environment/2020/12/in-rare-show-of-solidarity-14-key-nations-commit-to-protect-oceans/" style="font-size: 16px; font-style: normal; font-weight: 400;">rare show of solidarity</a><span style="font-size: 16px; font-style: normal; font-weight: 400;">", providing a bright spot in the </span><a href="https://www.iddri.org/en/publications-and-events/blog-post/2020-super-year-ocean" style="font-size: 16px;">Ocean super Year</a> that never was. </div>

</div>
<img src="/images/letters/sustainable_ocean_pillars.png" style="font-weight: 400; font-style: normal; font-size: 16px; outline-color: rgb(236, 33, 38); width: 500px; max-width: 500px;display:block;float:none;clear:both;height:auto;max-width:100%;margin:0.75rem auto;" width="500"/>
<div style="font-size: 16px;">
<p><span style="font-size: 16px;">Many of the world's maritime powers are conspicuously absent from the Panel, e.g. France, the USA and Russia. This is </span>not by accident<span style="font-size: 16px;">: “Negotiation with that category of country isn’t all that easy... </span><span style="font-size: 16px; font-style: normal; font-weight: 400;">We decided to get a group where high politics wouldn’t get in the way and we could be focused on the task”</span><span style="font-size: 16px;"> - Vidar Helgesen, the former Norwegian Minister of Climate and Environment who has been a driving force behind the initiative.</span></p>
<div style="font-size: 16px;">By forming a "coalition of the willing", the Panel is side-stepping the incrementalism of global consensus-building and leading by example. Given that these countries manage 40% of the world's coastline, their proactive and public commitment to ocean sustainability has the potential to generate real momentum.<br/>
<br/>
While the Panel does not shy away from calling for strong conservation action, they also stress need to sustainably manage all marine spaces and highlight the human dimension of ocean sustainability: <a href="https://www.oceanpanel.org/blue-papers/future-food-sea">increasing protein supply</a> for a growing population, implementing <a href="https://www.wri.org/blog/2020/07/investments-ocean-health-wealth">nature-based solutions</a> to limit temperature rise, and ending <a href="https://advances.sciencemag.org/content/4/7/e1701833">forced labour</a> and other human rights abuses.</div>

<hr/><span style="font-weight: bolder; font-size: 16px;">Ocean update</span>
<ul style="font-size: 16px;">
<li style="font-size: 16px;">Members of the Inter-American Tropical Tuna Commission (IATTC) <a href="https://www.undercurrentnews.com/2020/12/09/tuna-buyer-alliance-calls-for-emergency-iattc-measures/">failed to agree measures</a> for the management of tropical tunas (nor did they agree to maintain current measures). This leaves tropical tunas unmanaged, in clear contradiction to the IATTC's mandate, which is to... manage tropical tunas. The Global Tuna Alliance, a group of major suppliers and retailers, said it is "astonished" and "profoundly disappointed and frustrated".</li>
<li style="font-size: 16px;"><span style="font-size: 16px;">Two new reports consider the <a href="https://www.bluemarinefoundation.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/Entering-the-Twilight-Zone-Final.pdf">ecology of mesopelagic fishes</a> and the <a href="https://www.iddri.org/en/publications-and-events/study/fishing-twilight-zone-illuminating-governance-challenges-next">potential governance challenges</a> of commercial exploitation. A recording of a webinar launching the reports is available <a href="https://www.iddri.org/en/publications-and-events/conference/replay-fishing-twilight-zone-illuminating-governance-challenges">here</a>. </span></li>
</ul>
