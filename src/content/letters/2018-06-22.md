<span style="color: rgb(85, 85, 85); font-size: 16px; font-style: normal; font-weight: 400;">Isle de Jean Charles in Louisiana has already lost 98% of its land to erosion, subsidence, </span><span style="color: rgb(85, 85, 85); font-size: 16px; font-style: normal; font-weight: 400;">and a rising sea. In 2016, a $48-million subsidy was given to <a href="http://isledejeancharles.la.gov/">relocate residents</a>, effectively making them the first </span><span style="color: rgb(85, 85, 85); font-size: 16px; font-style: normal; font-weight: 400;">government-recognized climate refugees in the US. Now, a <a href="https://www.hakaimagazine.com/news/virtual-reality-preserves-disappearing-land/">v</a></span><a href="https://www.hakaimagazine.com/news/virtual-reality-preserves-disappearing-land/">irtual reality project</a> is aiming to preserve the <span style="color: rgb(85, 85, 85); font-size: 16px; font-style: normal; font-weight: 400;">Biloxi-Chitimacha-Choctaw people’s culture</span> too. <em>My visit with Billiot</em> is a unique VR archive of the area's rich oral history - take a look <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=evvrqBWMsG8">here</a>.<br/>

<hr/><strong>From the darkness...</strong><br/>
Elsewhere, new technology is lighting up dark fishing fleets. Illegal, unreported, and unregulated fishing (IUU) has a <a href="https://www.fishforward.eu/en/topics/facts-figures/">huge impact on marine life</a> and constitutes an estimated 11-26 million tonnes (12-28%) of fishing world-wide. Monitoring boats fishing at night has been one of many challenges, but last week <a href="http://globalfishingwatch.org/">Global Fishing Watch</a> revealed a new <a href="http://globalfishingwatch.org/map/workspace/udw-v2-9c50e1da-499c-40c1-80ed-0dddb88b085e">imaging system</a> capable of detecting vessels that fish at night.

<hr/><strong>Beyond Blue</strong><br/>
A new ocean exploration video game was unveiled last week at the E3 gaming convention. In <a href="https://beyondbluegame.com/"><em>Beyond Blue</em></a>, the player takes the role of Mirai, a research team leader that sets out on an expedition to see the ocean in a more meaningful way. Look out for its release in early 2019. In the meantime, check out the <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FV_wHEYUAF0">trailer</a>.
