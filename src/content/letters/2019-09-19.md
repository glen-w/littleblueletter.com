<div style="font-weight: 400; font-style: normal; font-size: 16px; color: rgb(85, 85, 85);"><span style="font-weight: bolder; font-style: normal; font-size: 16px;">Seaweed invasion</span>
<div dir="ltr">Pristine beaches, clear waters, palm trees, sea turtles... and a massive brown carpet of foul-smelling algae. An <a href="https://www.bbc.com/news/world-latin-america-45044513">invasion of dead Sargassum seaweed</a> is destroying marine life and causing significant losses to fishermen and the tourist industry in Caribbean islands and the <a href="https://www.weforum.org/agenda/2019/07/a-550-km-long-mass-of-rotting-seaweed-is-heading-for-mexicos-pristine-beaches/">Gulf of Mexico</a>. <br/>
<img src="/images/letters/inferred-2019-09-19-1-sargassumturtle.png" style="width: 350px; max-width: 350px;display:block;float:none;clear:both;height:auto;max-width:100%;margin:0.75rem auto;" width="350"/></div>
The stringy brown seaweed is usually found floating in large mats in the Sargasso Sea, where it <a href="https://www.smithsonianmag.com/science-nature/where-do-newly-hatched-baby-sea-turtles-go-180949954/">provides vital breeding and feeding grounds</a> for marine life. It is not yet clear what has caused the disproportionately huge bloom, but global warming and agricultural runoffs are considered important factors.

<div>
<p dir="ltr"><img src="/images/letters/inferred-2019-09-19-2-sargassum2.png" style="width: 487px; max-width: 487px;display:block;float:none;clear:both;height:auto;max-width:100%;margin:0.75rem auto;" width="487"/>When washed ashore at beaches, seaweed mats as deep as seven meters start to decompose, causing damage to <a href="https://www.facebook.com/thebstp/">sea turtle populations</a> whose nesting sites become blocked or damaged by removal work. In the recent <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l-LfqabPjNI&amp;feature=youtu.be">UNCTAD Raul Prebisch Lecture</a> (presented by a woman for the first time!), the Prime Minister of Barbados, Mia Mottley, warned the global community of the growing problem, highlighting a lack of resources for small islands to cope with this challenge. </p>
<div><img src="/images/letters/inferred-2019-09-19-3-capturedecran2019-09-19a223708.png" style="width: 398px; max-width: 398px;display:block;float:none;clear:both;height:auto;max-width:100%;margin:0.75rem auto;" width="398"/>
<p dir="ltr">Fortunately, people are finding ingenious solutions. Barbados has repurposed old sugar cane trucks to pick sargassum without removing sand from the beaches. A company in Guadeloupe has developed a boat equipped with a conveyor belt to collect the weeds quickly. From Plant tonics to <a href="http://www.thegef.org/news/communities-innovate-address-sargassum-seaweed-coasts-saint-lucia">fertilizers</a> to <a href="https://www.oldharbournews.com/content/using-sargassum-make-goat-feed-old-harbour-company-represent-jamaica-world%E2%80%99s-largest-green">goat feed</a>, other commercial uses are being explored.</p>

<img src="/images/letters/inferred-2019-09-19-5-capturedecran2019-09-26a181149.png" style="width: 465px; max-width: 465px;display:block;float:none;clear:both;height:auto;max-width:100%;margin:0.75rem auto;" width="465"/>
<ul>
<li dir="ltr">The tiny Central American country of Belize is a <a href="https://www.theguardian.com/environment/2019/aug/14/why-tiny-belize-is-a-world-leader-in-ocean-protection">world leader in protecting the ocean</a>. In close collaboration with the fishing community, a managed access fishing programme restricts ‘open access’ fishing through the distribution of licenses, thereby reducing pressure on vital fish stocks and the incentive to fish illegally.  </li>
<li>Salmon farming in the Patagonian Sea is facing a growing wave of resistance due to its <a href="http://marpatagonico.org/en/patagonian-sea/values/">environmentally damaging and polluting practices</a>. Campaigners have now put a stop to one company’s plans to rear 1.9m fish in the <a href="https://www.theguardian.com/environment/2019/aug/30/salmon-farming-in-the-beagle-channel-enters-troubled-waters">Beagle Channel</a> between Chile and Argentina.</li>
</ul>
