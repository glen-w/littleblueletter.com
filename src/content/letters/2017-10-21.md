<hr/>
<div style="text-align: left;">The climate conference in Bonn wraps up today. Hosts Fiji <a href="https://cop23.com.fj/the-ocean-pathway/">launched</a> their Ocean Pathway initiative yesterday, building on the <a href="https://www.un.org/ga/search/view_doc.asp?symbol=A/RES/71/312&amp;Lang=E">Call for Action</a> from the UN Ocean Conference. The <a href="https://cop23.com.fj/wp-content/uploads/2017/11/The-Ocean-Pathway-Strategy-8.11.2017.pdf">Pathway</a> "flags the possibility of a UNFCCC agenda item and a work programme by 2019 and ensures the insertion of the ocean into NDCs" (Nationally Determined Contributions - the climate commitments made by States).<br/>
<br/>
The International Commission for the Conservation of Atlantic Tunas (ICCAT) is also meeting this week, and many States want to <a href="https://www.nytimes.com/aponline/2017/11/14/world/europe/ap-eu-threatened-fish-.html">increase the quota</a> of bluefin tuna, which is slowly starting to show signs of recovery. Here's a <a href="http://www.pewtrusts.org/en/research-and-analysis/blogs/compass-points/2017/11/09/faq-tunas-sharks-have-much-to-gain-or-lose-at-fisheries-meeting">handy FAQ</a>. (NB: campaigners call ICCAT the 'International Cartel to Catch All Tuna', but you didn't hear that from me!)<br/>
<br/>
Open Communications for the Ocean (OCTO) just launched a new, free repository of marine science - <a href="https://www.marxiv.org">MarXiv</a> (rhymes with archive) - which aims to "give managers and planners free access to the science they need, which is usually otherwise hidden behind expensive journal fees". Consider <a href="https://www.marxiv.org/submission">posting pre-prints</a> of your papers!

UNESCO's International Oceanographic Commission (IOC) recently released the <a href="https://en.unesco.org/gosr">Global Ocean Science Report</a>, which assesses the status and trends in ocean science capacity around the world. The report offers a global record of who, how, and where ocean science is conducted.</span><br/>
<br/>
Check out <a href="http://playground.qlik.com/liveshowcase/decode-oct-2017/index.html">this map</a> of commitments made at the UN Oceans Conference in June. <br/>

World Ocean Radio has a nice set of audio essays on the future of the Arctic, looking at <a href="http://worldoceanobservatory.org/wor/arctic-futures-part-one-governance-oversight-and-natura-resources">governance</a>, the <a href="http://worldoceanobservatory.org/wor/arctic-futures-part-two-arctic-council">Arctic Council</a>, <a href="http://worldoceanobservatory.org/wor/arctic-futures-part-three-climate-change-and-adaptation">climate change</a>, and the health and welfare of <a href="http://worldoceanobservatory.org/wor/arctic-futures-part-four-health-and-welfare-indigenous-people-arctic">Indigenous people</a>.
