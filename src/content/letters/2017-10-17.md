<hr/>Last year, members of the Commission for the Conservation of Antarctic Marine Living Resources (CCAMLR) reached a <a href="https://www.ccamlr.org/node/92518">landmark agreement</a> to create a marine protected area (MPA) in the Ross Sea. CCAMLR is now meeting again, with France &amp; Australia <a href="http://www.abc.net.au/news/2017-10-16/optimism-agreement-will-be-reached-to-protect-east-antarctic-wa/9054788">pushing for another MPA</a> in East Antartica (<a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3kbc4C_vWbk">video</a>). The meeting comes after a <a href="http://www.bbc.com/news/science-environment-41608722">catastrophic breeding season</a> for Antarctica's emblematic Adélie penguins.<br/>
<br/>
Tuesday was International Sawfish Day - check out <a href="http://twitter.com/SharkTrustUK/status/920216718047432704">this </a><a href="https://twitter.com/SharkTrustUK/status/920216718047432704">infographic</a>, this wonderful <a href="https://twitter.com/ZSLLibrary/status/920226973410095104">old drawing</a>, and some cool art from ancient <a href="https://twitter.com/AlecBMMoore/status/920215510809268225">Iraq</a> &amp; <a href="https://twitter.com/Africas_Sawfish/status/920256377863499777">A</a><a href="http://twitter.com/Africas_Sawfish/status/920256377863499777">ustralia</a>. See the saw in action <a href="https://i.imgur.com/nJE2gA5.gif">here</a>.<img src="/images/letters/inferred-2017-10-17-1-sawfish.jpg" style="width: 451px; max-width: 451px;display:block;float:none;clear:both;height:auto;max-width:100%;margin:0.75rem auto;" width="451"/><span style="color: rgb(85, 85, 85); font-size: 16px; font-style: normal; font-weight: normal;">Plastic is turning up <a href="http://oceana.org/blog/plastic-everything-now-here-are-seven-places-it%E2%80%99s-turned-lately" style="font-size: 16px;">everywhere</a>, and a <a href="https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2017/09/170925104730.htm" style="font-size: 16px;">new study</a> suggests that tiny particles can be absorbed into the brains of marine creatures and change their behaviour. But the tide is turning. In the UK, businesses are </span>committing to <a href="http://www.theguardian.com/world/2017/oct/16/high-street-outlets-move-to-ditch-plastic-amid-environmental-concerns">phase out</a> single use plastics<span style="font-weight: normal; font-style: normal; font-size: 16px; color: rgb(85, 85, 85);"> and</span><span style="font-weight: normal; font-style: normal; font-size: 16px; color: rgb(85, 85, 85);"> the ex-head of Asda recently <a href="https://www.theguardian.com/environment/2017/oct/12/supermarkets-stop-using-plastic-packaging-former-asda-boss-andy-clarke" style="font-size: 16px;">railed against</a> wasteful packaging (the packaging industry was <a href="https://www.packagingnews.co.uk/news/ex-asda-chief-calls-plastic-packaging-rethink-13-10-2017" style="font-size: 16px;">not impressed</a>, surprise surprise). Chile is </span><a href="http://smallchangegiving.co/submissions/chile-to-be-the-first-american-country-to-ban-plastic-bags-in-coastal-cities" style="font-weight: normal; font-style: normal; font-size: 16px;">banning</a><span style="font-weight: normal; font-style: normal; font-size: 16px; color: rgb(85, 85, 85);"> plastic bags in coastal cities, Seattle will </span><a href="https://www.ecowatch.com/seattle-ban-plastic-straws-2476937005.html" style="font-weight: normal; font-style: normal; font-size: 16px;">ban</a> plastic straws &amp; cutlery<span style="color: rgb(85, 85, 85); font-size: 16px; font-style: normal; font-weight: normal;"> next year, and an Indonesian company is making <a href="https://www.fastcompany.com/40477587/instead-of-throwing-out-this-plastic-wrapper-you-eat-it">edible/biodegradable packaging</a> from seaweed. Time to <a href="https://stopsucking.strawlessocean.org/i/lh3mfqb" style="font-size: 16px;">#StopSucking</a> and <a href="https://twitter.com/TurnTidePlastic" style="font-size: 16px;">#TurnTheTideOnPlastic</a>!<br/>
<br/>
A </span><a href="https://www.sciencemag.org/news/2017/10/offshore-wind-farms-have-powerful-advantage-over-land-based-turbines-study-finds" style="font-weight: normal; font-style: normal; font-size: 16px;">new study</a><span style="color: rgb(85, 85, 85); font-size: 16px; font-style: normal; font-weight: normal;"> suggests that floating wind turbines at sea could generate up to 3 times as much electricity as turbines on land, just as Scotland opens the <a href="http://www.bbc.com/news/uk-scotland-41652707">world's first</a> floating wind farm and France </span><a href="https://www.connexionfrance.com/French-news/First-floating-wind-turbine-in-France-launched-today" style="font-weight: normal; font-style: normal; font-size: 16px;">launches</a><span style="color: rgb(85, 85, 85); font-size: 16px; font-style: normal; font-weight: normal;"> its first test project. In the future, we might also make </span><a href="http://www.whoi.edu/news-release/seaweed-fuel" style="font-weight: normal; font-style: normal; font-size: 16px;">fuel from seaweed</a><span style="color: rgb(85, 85, 85); font-size: 16px; font-style: normal; font-weight: normal;">. </span>Elsewhere, ocean creatures are inspiring everything from <a href="https://www.sciencenews.org/article/potential-drug-found-sea-creature-can-now-be-made-efficiently-lab">pharmaceuticals</a> to <a href="https://www.sciencenews.org/article/watch-cuttlefish-inspired-skin-morph-3-d-shape">3D camouflage</a>.
