<hr/><span style="font-weight: bolder;">Ocean update</span><br/>
Do you want the good news or the bad news? We try our best to keep the <em>Letter</em> light-hearted - a tough task when every day seems to bring yet more cause for concern. This week, scientists have reported that increasingly regular and intense ocean heat waves are causing whales in California to get <a href="https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/ocean-heat-waves-linked-to-rise-in-whale-entanglements">entangled in fishing gear</a> more often (because they feed closer to shore when the water warms up) and that an acidic Pacific is <a href="https://www.forbes.com/sites/trevornace/2020/01/28/the-pacific-ocean-is-so-acidic-that-its-dissolving-dungeness-crabs">not good for dungeness crabs</a>.<br/>
<img src="/images/letters/inferred-2020-01-30-1-seahorse.gif" style="width: 444px; max-width: 444px;display:block;float:none;clear:both;height:auto;max-width:100%;margin:0.75rem auto;" width="444"/>
<div style="text-align: center;"><em>Dungeness crabs are dissolving, but are they cute enough for us to care? (© Shutterstock)</em></div>
<br/>
The good news:
<ul>
<li><a href="https://www.newscientist.com/article/2231442-albatrosses-strapped-with-sensors-help-spy-on-illegal-fishing-boats/">Albatross carrying radar sensors</a> have been used to monitor fishing boats in the Indian Ocean, revealing that a quarter of vessels are operating illegally.</li>
<li>The UK is spending £2.5 million to <a href="https://www.oceanographicmagazine.com/natural-england-seagrass-meadows/">restore fragile marine habitats</a> like seagrass meadows (<a href="https://twitter.com/MarinePolicy/status/1223191485719420928">but let's not mention the B-word</a>).</li>
<li>Tonga just finished a <a href="https://www.sciencecodex.com/global-reef-expedition-kingdom-tonga-639516">massive stocktake</a> of its marine ecosystems, finding that corals there are "moderately healthy".</li>
<li>Iceland seems to be <a href="https://therevelator.org/iceland-whaling/">losing its taste for whale meat</a> (there were 0 whale hunts last year).</li>
<li><a href="https://www.oceanoculus.com/news-from-the-sea/campaign-fishfreefeb">"Fish Free February"</a> will encourage people to give up seafood for a month.</li>
</ul>
