<div style="font-weight: normal; font-style: normal; font-size: 16px; color: rgb(85, 85, 85);"><span style="font-weight: bolder; font-style: normal;">The answer is blowing in the wind</span><br/>
Capturing just 2% of the world’s wind energy could meet all our electricity needs, according to a <a href="http://www.pnas.org/content/114/43/11338.full">new study</a>. Yet we still rely <a href="http://www.worldenergy.org/wp-content/uploads/2016/10/World-Energy-Resources-Full-report-2016.10.03.pdf">almost exclusively</a> on fossil fuels (oil, coal and gas accounted for around 86% in 2015; only 1,44% came from wind).<br/>
<br/>
Wind turbines are becoming ever more efficient, and we are slowly figuring out how best to store and distribute the energy that renewables produce. But at 80m tall and with blades bigger than an Airbus A380, not everybody is enthusiastic. W<span style="font-size: 16px; font-style: normal; font-weight: normal;">hereas </span><a href="https://www.theguardian.com/commentisfree/2017/nov/11/brighton-rampion-wind-farm-turbines-renewables" style="font-size: 16px; font-style: normal; font-weight: normal;">this journalist learned to love</a><span style="font-size: 16px; font-style: normal; font-weight: normal;"> her local offshore turbines, one </span><span style="font-size: 16px; font-style: normal; font-weight: normal;">UK wind farm will soon be </span><a href="https://www.thetimes.co.uk/article/end-of-wind-turbine-blight-after-lake-district-campaign-kirkby-moor-on-the-furness-peninsula-friends-of-the-lake-district-open-spaces-society-ff8l673p5" style="font-size: 16px; font-style: normal; font-weight: normal;">dismantled</a><span style="font-size: 16px; font-style: normal; font-weight: normal;"> following a campaign to remove this 'blight' from the landscape.</span></div>
<div style="font-weight: normal; font-style: normal; font-size: 16px; color: rgb(85, 85, 85);"><a href="https://handlemanpost.wordpress.com/2014/01/23/superturbines-open-the-door-to-new-era-for-wind-power/"><img src="/images/letters/inferred-2017-12-08-1-toothbrush.png" style="width: 459px; max-width: 459px;display:block;float:none;clear:both;height:auto;max-width:100%;margin:0.75rem auto;" width="459"/></a>Nonetheless, global<span style="color: rgb(85, 85, 85); font-size: 16px; font-style: normal; font-weight: normal;"> </span><a href="http://setis.ec.europa.eu/sites/default/files/reports/wind_energy_status_report_2016.pdf" style="font-weight: normal; font-style: normal; font-size: 16px;">installed capacity</a> hit 64GW in 2015, a 20% increase from 2014. In 2016, wind was the largest recipient of investment in the power sector - <a href="https://windeurope.org/about-wind/reports/financing-investment-trends-2016/">€43bn in Europe</a>, where wind now covers <a href="https://windeurope.org/about-wind/statistics/european/wind-in-power-2016/">10.4% of electricity demand</a>. Wind could be <a href="https://www.theguardian.com/commentisfree/2017/sep/26/offshore-wind-power-energy-price-climate-change">as cheap or cheaper</a> than any other form of power generation by the 2020s, and even the more expensive offshore turbines could still be better value for money <a href="https://www.theguardian.com/commentisfree/2017/sep/13/the-guardian-view-of-offshore-wind-cheaper-and-greener">than nuclear</a>.

<span style="font-style: normal; font-size: 16px;"><span style="font-weight: bolder;">Bathroom blues</span></span><br/>
Personal question: when did you last replace your toothbrush? Colgate reckons you need a new one <a href="http://www.colgate.com/en-us/oral-health/basics/brushing-and-flossing/how-often-should-you-change-your-toothbrush-0114">every 3 months</a>. Just imagine if all 7.5 billion of us chewed through 4 disposable plastic toothbrushes each year (more money for Colgate; tonnes more plastic in the ocean). Time to <a href="http://bered-Bristles/dp/B00UYFH6YO/ref=as_li_ss_tl?ie=UTF8&amp;linkCode=sl1&amp;tag=cademiabscura-20&amp;linkId=8b974e08a8a4d443764560bb6f8267e0">switch to bamboo</a>: zero plastic, biodegradable, and looks awesome!<img src="/images/letters/inferred-2017-12-08-4-capturedecran2017-12-08a123903.png" style="font-weight: normal; font-style: normal; font-size: 16px; outline-color: rgb(236, 33, 38); width: 328px; max-width: 328px;display:block;float:none;clear:both;height:auto;max-width:100%;margin:0.75rem auto;" width="328"/>Now, imagine a world where your socks don’t get lost in the wash and your clothes don’t <a href="http://www.bbc.com/news/science-environment-40498292">shed half a million microfibres</a> into the sea. The future is now with the <a href="https://guppyfriend.langbrett.com/en/">GUPPYFRIEND Washing Bag</a>.

<hr/><span style="font-weight: bolder; font-size: 1em;">From the editor</span><br/>
<span style="color: rgb(85, 85, 85); font-size: 16px; font-style: normal; font-weight: normal;">The UN recently announced a </span><a href="https://en.unesco.org/ocean-decade" style="font-weight: normal; font-style: normal; font-size: 16px;">Decade of Ocean Science for Sustainable Development</a><span style="color: rgb(85, 85, 85); font-size: 16px; font-style: normal; font-weight: normal;">, aiming to produce a whole new generation of marine scientists. </span><span style="font-size: 1em;">The UN also passed the annual <a href="https://www.un.org/press/en/2017/ga11985.doc.htm">Resolution on Oceans</a> this week. </span>Plans to <a href="https://www.independent.co.uk/environment/un-ocean-plastic-waste-resolution-us-china-india-reject-pollution-sea-united-nations-environment-a8095541.html">set targets for ocean plastic</a> were scuttled by the US, China and India (even though <span style="font-size: 16px; font-style: normal; font-weight: normal;">UN Environment has '</span><a href="http://web.unep.org/newscentre/un-declares-war-ocean-plastic" style="font-size: 16px; font-style: normal; font-weight: normal;">declared war</a><span style="font-size: 16px; font-style: normal; font-weight: normal;">' on marine debris)</span><span style="font-size: 16px; font-style: normal; font-weight: normal;">. Some good news though: there is now huge support for a new treaty to protect the high seas (i.e. half the planet!). More on this next week.</span></div>
