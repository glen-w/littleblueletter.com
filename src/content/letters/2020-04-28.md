<hr/><span style="font-weight: bolder; font-size: 16px;">The pandemic and the Ocean</span>
<ul dir="ltr">
<li role="presentation" style="font-weight: 400; font-style: normal; font-size: 16px;">Marine creatures are enjoying some quiet time as <a href="https://www.theguardian.com/environment/2020/apr/27/silence-is-golden-for-whales-as-lockdown-reduces-ocean-noise-coronavirus" style="font-size: 16px;">underwater noise</a> levels drop. Scientists are studying these effects on marine mammals.</li>
<li role="presentation" style="font-weight: 400; font-style: normal; font-size: 16px;">​From <a href="https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2020/apr/19/florida-leatherback-turtles-coronavirus-beaches" style="font-size: 16px;">Florida</a> to <a href="https://edition.cnn.com/travel/article/thailand-sea-turtles-coronavirus-scli-intl-scn/index.html" style="font-size: 16px;">Thailand</a>, the number of sea turtles nests has increased on the now-empty beaches. The rapid recovery of marine wildlife in coastal areas shows how extensive our impacts are and highlights the importance of protected areas. </li>
<li role="presentation" style="font-weight: 400; font-style: normal; font-size: 16px;"><span style="font-size: 16px;">Fishers around the world are struggling with decreased demand, lack of sanitary conditions and logistical challenges. In some countries, like </span><a href="https://www.hakaimagazine.com/news/indias-fishers-have-been-crushed-by-covid-19/" style="font-size: 16px;">India</a><span style="font-size: 16px;">, food security of the communities may be affected by this disruption of supply chains.</span></li>
<li role="presentation" style="font-weight: 400; font-style: normal; font-size: 16px;"><span style="color: rgb(85, 85, 85); font-size: 16px; font-style: normal; font-weight: 400;">PADI and Rash’R are producing (non-profit) </span><a href="https://www.scubadivermag.com/padi-partners-with-rashr-to-make-eco-friendly-reusable-face-masks/" style="font-weight: 400; font-style: normal; font-size: 16px;">reusable face masks made from Ocean plastic</a><span style="color: rgb(85, 85, 85); font-size: 16px; font-style: normal; font-weight: 400;">, with designs based on sea animals!</span></li>
</ul>

<hr/><strong>World Penguin Day</strong><br/>
25 April was World Penguin Day! <span style="color: rgb(85, 85, 85); font-size: 16px; font-style: normal; font-weight: 400;">There are 18 <a href="http://www.nationalgeographic.com/animals/birds/group/penguins/">different species of penguins</a>. They are one of the most endangered </span><span style="color: rgb(85, 85, 85); font-size: 16px; font-style: normal; font-weight: 400;">seabirds, with two thirds on the IUCN Red List. That's why the Pew Charitable Trust is campaigning for <a href="https://www.pewtrusts.org/en/research-and-analysis/articles/2020/04/24/world-penguin-day-highlights-need-for-more-southern-ocean-protections">more Southern Ocean protections</a>.</span>
