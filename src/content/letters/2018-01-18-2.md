<div style="font-weight: normal; font-style: normal; font-size: 16px; color: rgb(85, 85, 85);"><span style="font-weight: bolder; font-style: normal; font-size: 16px;">Whale highway</span><br/>
At the 2017 Our Ocean conference, the Spanish Government <a href="https://www.ourocean2017.org/sites/default/files/ooc-2017-list-of-commitments_en.pdf">committed</a> to designate almost 5% of its waters as a “Cetacean migration corridor of the Mediterranean”. The government is set to officially announce the marine protected area (MPA) within the next 6 months. The corridor will cover 46,000 square kilometres of the Mediterranean Sea – about half the size of Portugal. With this new MPA, 13% of Spanish waters will be protected.<br/>
<img src="/images/letters/giphy-mW05nwEyXLP0Y.gif" style="width: 357px; max-width: 357px;display:block;float:none;clear:both;height:auto;max-width:100%;margin:0.75rem auto;" width="357"/><a href="https://alianzamarblava.org/en/">Aliança Mar Blava</a>, an intersectoral platform comprising a range of stakeholders and institutions, has been actively promoting the initiative since 2015, <a href="https://alianzamarblava.org/es/comunicados/proteccion-corredor-migracion-cetaceos-como-zepim-antes-navidad-manos-convenio-barcelona/">arguing</a> that an MPA is needed to reduce pressure from human activities (fishing, maritime traffic, etc.). The area will provide habitat and a migratory corridor for a range of mammals, including fin and sperm whales, as well as deep divers, such as pilot whales and Cuvier's beaked whales.<br/>
<img src="/images/letters/inferred-2018-01-18-2-1-docx-littleblueletterjanuary2018gwedit-5.png" style="width: 289px; max-width: 289px;display:block;float:none;clear:both;height:auto;max-width:100%;margin:0.75rem auto;" width="289"/>

Orange &amp; Yellow: Request for research permit.</em></div>
<div style="text-align: center;"> </div>
Crucially, the protection regime will completely prohibit fossil fuel exploitation and harmful seismic exploration. The Spanish government must therefore <a href="https://alianzamarblava.org/en/statements/barcelona-convention-gives-international-protection-cetacean-migration-corridor/">shelve all ongoing hydrocarbon prospection</a> and applications for hydrocarbon research permits. This move has not been without <a href="https://elpais.com/elpais/2014/02/18/inenglish/1392716535_597459.html">controversy</a>. But in the end, the decision is good news for whales, and for the <a href="http://www.diariodemallorca.es/mallorca/2014/04/13/millar-personas-suma-marea-negra/925466.html">thousands of concerned citizens</a> who rallied against the development of offshore oil and gas.

<hr/><strong>EU joins the war on plastic waste</strong><br/>
The first <a href="http://europa.eu/rapid/press-release_IP-18-5_en.htm">European strategy on plastics</a> was adopted this week. The strategy takes a “circular economy” approach, focussing on transforming the entire plastics industry. The aim is to make recycling profitable, drive investment and innovation and, ultimately, stop marine waste.<span style="font-weight: normal; font-style: normal; font-size: 16px;"> </span>
<hr/>
<div style="font-style: normal; font-size: 16px;"><span style="font-weight: bolder; font-style: normal; font-size: 16px;">Bathroom blues</span></div>
Around 25.8 million tonnes of plastic waste are generated in Europe every year. In previous editions, we’ve mentioned <a href="http://amzn.to/2DOc15n">bamboo toothbrushes</a> and the <a href="http://guppyfriend.com/en/">GUPPYFRIEND Washing Bag</a>. This week, we look inside your ears (figuratively speaking).<br/>
<br/>
Disposable cotton buds with plastic handles quickly <a href="https://twitter.com/FrancesLumsden/status/951484333117853696">clog waste water systems</a>, choke marine life, and are one of the most common items found on beaches. <a href="https://www.independent.co.uk/environment/plastic-cotton-bud-ban-scotland-manufacturing-latest-a8156741.html">Scotland’s decision to ban them completely</a> should therefore come as no surprise. You can do your bit by <a href="http://amzn.to/2DNfsZG">switching to paper cotton buds</a>.
