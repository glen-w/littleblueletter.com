**🌊 Geoengineering finally gets a rulebook**
You can't just dump iron in the ocean and call it a climate solution! That's what countries at the International Maritime Organization (IMO) [agreed this week](https://www.imo.org/en/mediacentre/pressbriefings/pages/imo-adopts-marine-geoengineering-frameworks-for-research-activities.aspx) (sort of). They adopted new frameworks for assessing marine geoengineering research proposals, grounded in a precautionary approach. Proposals must demonstrate a legitimate scientific purpose, with thorough risk assessment, stakeholder consultation, peer review, monitoring and reporting.

**🌀 Momentum building for protection of the Sargasso Sea**
A new [Hamilton Declaration](https://www.gov.bm/news/uk-minister-for-the-overseas-territories-to-visit-bermuda) will be signed next week in Bermuda. It's a step towards international cooperation on high-seas biodiversity, and bolsters a future collective proposal for protections under the BBNJ Agreement.

**💰 Securing long-term financing for the Western Indian Ocean**
At a meeting of the Nairobi Convention (which is celebrating its [40th birthday](https://www.nairobiconvention.org/news/cop12-40th-anniversary-western-indian-ocean)!), countries [decided to develop](https://www.project-syndicate.org/commentary/ocean-conservation-trust-fund-to-facilitate-cross-border-cooperation-by-marie-may-jeremie-2026-10) a regional conservation trust fund to build sustainable funding beyond the usual piecemeal efforts. Because, as Seychelles Environment Minister Marie-May Jeremie put it: *"Reefs do not recover according to project deadlines."*

**🐋 China protects habitat for Bryde's whales**
The waters off Weizhou Island now have a dedicated 335 km² Bryde's whale habitat, with fishing banned for most of the year. It's a first for large-whale habitat protection on China's coast. [ECNS](https://www.ecns.cn/m/cns-wire/2026-10-13/detail-ihfmcrte5284873.shtml)
